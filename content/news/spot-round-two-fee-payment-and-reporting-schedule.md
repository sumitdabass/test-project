## Spot Round 2 Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official schedule for fee payment and institute reporting under Online Spot Round 2 for the 2026–27 academic session.

Candidates participating in the counselling process can track updates on the university admission portal and consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for procedural steps.

### Applicable Programmes

The schedule covers admissions through National Level Tests (NLT) and the Common Entrance Test (CET) for the following undergraduate courses:

* Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) — see details on [GGSIPU counselling for B.Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php)
* Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
* Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
* Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) [B.Com (H)]
* Bachelor of Laws (LLB)

## Actions Required

All candidates allotted seats in Spot Round 2 must complete the requisite fee payment online and report to their allotted institutes within the specified timeline. For exact dates, session timings, and reporting instructions, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in.
