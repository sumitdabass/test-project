## Official Advisory Against Admission Fraud

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a public notice warning prospective students, candidates, and parents to remain vigilant against touts and unauthorized middlemen. These fraudulent entities may falsely claim to guarantee admission into various university programs for the 2026-27 academic year.

The university has clarified that it has not authorized any external agency, consultant, or individual to facilitate or guarantee admissions.

## Merit-Based Admission Process

All admissions at GGSIPU are conducted strictly in accordance with established merit guidelines. Depending on the program, selection is based on:

* Performance in national-level entrance examinations.
* Performance in the university's own Common Entrance Test (CET).
* Participation in the official, centralized online counselling process.

For a secure and step-by-step understanding of the official enrollment procedures, candidates can consult our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Guidelines for Candidates and Parents

To avoid falling victim to admission scams, stakeholders are advised to adhere to the following instructions:

* **Avoid Third Parties:** Do not engage with anyone offering direct admission or management quota seats through unofficial channels.
* **Verify Information:** Rely solely on the official university portals (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in) for all admission notifications, seat matrices, and fee payment details.
* **Report Fraud:** Any suspicious approaches or demands for money in exchange for guaranteed seats should be reported directly to the university administration or local law enforcement.
