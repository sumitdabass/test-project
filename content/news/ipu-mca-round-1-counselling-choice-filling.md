## MCA (Code 105) Round 1 Counselling Begins

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially commenced the Round 1 centralized online counselling process for the Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program, under CET Code 105, for the academic session 2026-27.

Eligible candidates can now participate in the enrollment process, access the edit/correction window, and begin choice filling.

## Key Activities in Round 1

The current phase of the centralized online counselling includes the following key steps:

*   **Counselling Enrollment:** Registration and enrollment for the centralized online counselling process.
*   **Edit/Correction Window:** Candidates can modify or correct details in their application forms if required.
*   **Choice Filling:** Candidates must fill in their preferred choices of colleges and institutes offering the MCA program in order of preference.

For a comprehensive understanding of the admission process, eligibility criteria, and college options, you can refer to our detailed [IPU MCA Admission Guide](/mca-admission-ipu.php).

## Important Instructions for Candidates

*   **Choice Locking:** Ensure that you save and lock your choices before the specified deadline. Unlocked choices may not be considered for allotment.
*   **Document Verification:** Keep all necessary academic and category documents ready for verification during the counselling rounds.
*   **Official Notification:** Candidates are advised to download the official PDF notification from the IPU admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in) to verify the exact start and end dates for choice filling and corrections.
