## M.Ed (CET Code 120) First Round Counselling Details

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially announced the schedule for the first round of offline counselling and admissions for the Master of Education (M.Ed) program under CET Code 120. This schedule is applicable for the academic session 2026-27.

The official notification was published on June 15, 2026. Because this is an offline counselling process, qualified candidates must physically report to the designated venue according to their rank-wise schedule.

### Key Highlights of the Notification
* **Program:** Master of Education (M.Ed)
* **CET Code:** 120
* **Counselling Mode:** Offline (Physical reporting required)
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Notification Date:** June 15, 2026

For general instructions on navigating the university's admission process, candidates can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Instructions for Candidates

Candidates planning to participate in the offline counselling must ensure they carry all necessary documents, including their CET admit card, rank card, academic certificates, and the required demand draft for fees. 

Because specific reporting dates, times, and venue details are detailed in the official PDF, candidates are strongly advised to download the complete schedule directly from the official GGSIPU admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in).
