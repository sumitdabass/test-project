## MBBS Special Stray Round Notification

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of subsequent activities for candidates participating in the Special Stray Round for MBBS (Code 103) admissions for the 2026-27 academic year.

This notification outlines the next procedural steps required following the declaration of results for this counselling round.

## Important Details for Candidates

- **Programme:** MBBS (Code 103)
- **Academic Year:** 2026-27
- **Round:** Special Stray Round
- **Subject:** Schedule of activities pursuant to result declaration

Candidates who have participated in the Special Stray Round should consult the official university portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in to verify the exact deadlines for allotment acceptance, document submission, and institutional reporting.
