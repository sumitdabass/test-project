## GGSIPU Spot Round Counselling 2026-27

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has released an official notice regarding the online spot round of counselling for several undergraduate and postgraduate professional programmes for the 2026-27 academic session.

### Eligible Programmes

The online spot round counselling schedule covers the following academic courses:

* B.Tech and B.Tech (Lateral Entry)
* Integrated BA-LLB and BBA-LLB
* BBA and 5-Year Integrated BBA-MBA
* BCA
* B.Ed
* B.Com (Hons)
* BA (Economics)
* LLM

For details on engineering admissions, visit the [GGSIPU B.Tech Counselling guide](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) or consult the overall [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Applicants

Candidates who wish to participate in the spot round counselling must register on the official counselling portal and complete choice filling as per the prescribed timelines. For specific dates, eligibility criteria, and fee structures, refer to the official notification available on ipu.ac.in.
