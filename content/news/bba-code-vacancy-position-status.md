## BBA Vacancy Status Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has published the latest vacancy position for admission to its Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme, designated under CET Code 125. The data outlines the seat availability across affiliated institutes as on August 31, 2026.

Students tracking the counselling process can consult this list to understand which colleges and categories still have seats open before subsequent rounds or spot rounds.

## Reviewing the Vacancy List

Candidates participating in GGSIPU admissions should review the notification carefully on the official admission portal. For a broader overview of counselling stages and seat allotment procedures, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). Those assessing institute options relative to past closing ranks may also refer to previous data via the [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) resources.

### Official Verification

Seat distributions and institute-wise category breakdowns are detailed in the official PDF document. For full details and further schedule updates, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in.
