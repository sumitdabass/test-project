## Extended Schedule for MBBS Round 1

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has notified an extended schedule of activities for candidates following the declaration of Round 1 results for the MBBS programme (Programme Code 103) for the academic session 2026-27.

### What Candidates Need to Know

- **Programme:** MBBS (Code 103)
- **Counselling Phase:** Round 1 post-result activities
- **Academic Session:** 2026-27

Candidates participating in the medical admissions process should refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for standard university procedures and verify all specific extended deadlines directly from the official notification on ipu.admissions.nic.in.
