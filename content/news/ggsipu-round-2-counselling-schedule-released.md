## Round 2 Counselling Activities Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities following the declaration of the Round 02 counselling results. This schedule is applicable for the academic session 2026-27 and covers several undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Candidates who participated in the second round of counselling must check their allotment status and strictly adhere to the timelines specified by the university for subsequent procedures.

## Covered Programs

The newly released schedule applies to the following courses:
* Bachelor of Education (B.Ed)
* BA (Journalism & Mass Communication)
* B.Com (Honours)
* BA English (Honours)
* BA Economics (Honours)
* Lateral Entry to B.Tech (LE B.Tech)
* Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
* Master of Laws (LLM)

## Next Steps for Allotted Candidates

Following the seat allotment, candidates are required to complete several critical steps within the designated timeframe. These steps typically include:

1. **Checking Allotment Status:** Log in to the official IPU admissions portal to view your seat status.
2. **Payment of Part Academic Fee:** Pay the required fee online to secure the allotted seat.
3. **Willingness Submission:** Choose to either 'Freeze' your allotted seat if satisfied, or 'Float' if you wish to participate in subsequent rounds.
4. **Document Verification:** Ensure all required documents are ready for verification as per the university guidelines.

For a detailed breakdown of the admission process, you can consult our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). If you are looking to compare previous admission trends, you may also review the [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) details.

Candidates are advised to download the official PDF from the GGSIPU admissions website to verify the exact closing dates and times for fee submission and seat freezing.
