## Paramedical CUET Merit List Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the CUET merit list for candidates seeking admission to various undergraduate paramedical courses for the 2026-27 academic session.

The document, dated 16 October 2026, provides candidate merit positions based on CUET scores for admission to allied health sciences programmes across participating institutions.

## Programmes Included in the Merit List

The released merit list applies to the following undergraduate allied health and paramedical disciplines:

- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.PT)
- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (B.OT)
- Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)
- Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science (BMLS)
- Bachelor of Prosthetics and Orthotics (BPO)

## Checking Your Position and Official Details

Applicants can access the detailed merit list PDF through the official admissions website at ipu.admissions.nic.in. For a general understanding of the university's admission frameworks and subsequent counseling processes, refer to our [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

Candidates are advised to refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in for comprehensive schedule details and subsequent counseling instructions.
