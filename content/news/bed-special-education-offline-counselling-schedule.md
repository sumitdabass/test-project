## Offline Counselling for B.Ed (Special Education)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification outlining the offline counselling schedule for admission to its B.Ed (Special Education) programme (Code 159) for the 2026-27 academic session.

Candidates who have qualified through the IPU CET, CUET, or SET merit routes are eligible to participate in the offline seat allotment process.

## Key Admission Details

- **Programme:** B.Ed (Special Education)
- **Programme Code:** 159
- **Academic Session:** 2026-27
- **Admission Channels:** CET, CUET, and SET qualified applicants
- **Mode of Counselling:** Offline

For broader details on university admission norms and general procedural requirements, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Applicants

Eligible candidates should download and review the official PDF circular directly from the admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in. The notification specifies reporting dates, session venues, required original documents, and the requisite fee payment modalities for completing offline verification and seat confirmation.
