## Offline Counselling Notification Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the offline counselling schedule for candidates seeking admission to various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes for the 2026–27 academic session.

Prospective students can review general admission procedures through our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Covered Programmes

According to the notification, the offline counselling round applies to the following courses:

- BA (English)
- BA (Economics)
- Integrated BA/BBA LLB
- Master of Laws (LLM)
- B.Com

## Next Steps for Applicants

Candidates participating in the offline counselling rounds must verify the specific reporting dates, venue details, and document verification guidelines directly in the official notification issued on ipu.admissions.nic.in before attending.
