## BA English Refund Notice Issued

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official refund order concerning applicants who withdrew their admissions from the Bachelor of Arts (English) programme (CET Code 184) for the 2026–27 academic session.

## Order Details

According to the university release, the details of the refund processing are as follows:

* **Programme:** BA (English) (Code 184)
* **Order Reference:** Refund Order No. 3929
* **Diary Reference:** Diary No. 3928
* **Order Date:** September 10, 2026
* **Academic Session:** 2026–27
* **Target Group:** Candidates who submitted withdrawal requests

## Verification and Next Steps

Candidates who withdrew their candidature for the BA English programme during the 2026–27 admission cycle should examine the official list on the university admissions portal to verify their refund status. For further details on transaction timelines or individual queries, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
