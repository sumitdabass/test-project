## BBA Fee Refund Order Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official refund notice (Order No. 3968, dated 06.10.2026) regarding the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA, Code 125) programme for the 2026–27 academic session.

The order applies to candidates who participated in the admission process but did not report online following their seat allotment. 

### Scope of the Notification

- **Programme:** BBA (CET Code 125)
- **Academic Session:** 2026–27
- **Target Group:** Candidates marked as "not reported" during the online reporting stage
- **Order Details:** Refund Order No. 3968 dated 06.10.2026

### Verifying Your Refund Status

Students who were allotted seats in the BBA programme but opted not to report should review the official document released on the portal. For further context on standard university processes, prospective students can consult our [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

To view the complete list of eligible roll numbers and processing instructions, refer directly to the notification document published at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
