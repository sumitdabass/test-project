## Final Offline Registration Window Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notification offering a final opportunity for offline registration for candidates seeking admission across various programmes.

This window is specifically designated for candidates who have not yet registered with the university. If you are exploring entry options, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for an overview of general requirements.

## Qualifying Exam Modes

According to the official circular dated September 7, 2026, the registration opportunity applies to various courses where admissions are determined through the merit of:

- National Level Tests (NLT)
- Common Entrance Tests (CET)
- Common University Entrance Test (CUET)

Students seeking technical seats should review the procedures for [GGSIPU counselling for B.Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) to understand how entry channels are organized.

## Important Directions for Applicants

This process is strictly offline and restricted solely to "not registered" candidates. Applicants must review the specific dates, reporting venues, and detailed instructions by downloading the official notification from the official admissions website at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
