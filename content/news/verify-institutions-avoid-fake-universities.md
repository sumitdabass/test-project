## Advisory on Higher Educational Institutions

A formal request has been issued to alert students regarding the importance of verifying Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs). This advisory aims to help prospective students avoid seeking admission in fake universities.

### Verification of Institutions

Students are encouraged to verify the status of any institution before finalizing their admission. This process is crucial to ensure that the degree-granting body is recognized and legitimate. For general guidance on the university's admission process, students may refer to the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Avoiding Fake Universities

The primary goal of this alert is to safeguard students from fraudulent institutions. Whether applying for [MBA admission](/mba-admission-ip-university.php) or [B.Tech admission](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php), verifying the HEI's credentials is a necessary step.

Refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in for more information on verifying educational institutions.
