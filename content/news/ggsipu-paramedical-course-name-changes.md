## Update on Paramedical Course Nomenclature

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced a change in the nomenclature of certain paramedical courses. This adjustment is being implemented for the Academic Session 2026-27 to ensure compliance with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) standards.

### Key Details of the Change

The university has modified the titles of specific programs to align with national regulatory requirements. Prospective students applying for paramedical streams should take note of these updated titles during the registration and [counselling process](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Impact on Applicants

While the course content and eligibility criteria generally remain consistent with RCI guidelines, the official degree or diploma name on the certificate will reflect the new nomenclature. For a detailed list of the specific courses affected, candidates are advised to refer to the official notification dated May 18, 2026, available on the university's admission portal.

Students interested in these programs can also review the [IPU CET cutoff 2025](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) to understand previous admission trends for paramedical sciences. For a comprehensive overview of the application steps, visit our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
