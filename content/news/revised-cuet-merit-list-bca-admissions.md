## Revised CUET Merit List for BCA (Code 114)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the revised merit list for candidates seeking admission to the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) programme (Programme Code 114) via CUET scores for the 2026–27 academic session.

Candidates who applied for BCA admissions using their CUET scores should check the updated list to verify their revised ranking and merit standing.

## Important Steps for Candidates

- Visit the official admissions portal to access the complete merit list PDF.
- Verify your roll number, application details, and updated rank in the list.
- Monitor official updates for subsequent counselling rounds and seat allotment schedules.

For more details on admission procedures and eligibility criteria across programmes, refer to the [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or check the official university portal at ipu.ac.in.
