## Official Warning Against Admission Fraud

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official advisory warning students and parents to beware of touts, middlemen, or unauthorized agencies promising guaranteed admissions for the academic session 2026-27. 

The university has clarified that all admissions are conducted strictly through the official merit-based process. No individual or external agency has been authorized to facilitate admissions through unfair means or backdoor entries.

## How GGSIPU Admissions Work

Prospective students should note that all legitimate admissions to undergraduate and postgraduate programs are processed through the official university channels. 

* Admissions are based purely on merit, determined by entrance exams like the IPU CET, national-level tests, or qualifying exam marks.
* All counselling procedures, seat allotments, and fee payments are managed online through the official GGSIPU portals.
* For a complete overview of the legitimate entry pathways, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## What to Do If Approached

If any individual or agency approaches you claiming they can secure a seat at GGSIPU in exchange for money or other favors, you should immediately report them to the university authorities. 

Candidates and parents are strongly advised to rely only on official notifications published on the official websites (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in) to avoid falling victim to financial fraud or losing an academic year.
