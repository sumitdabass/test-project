## First Round Offline Counselling for MA (English)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially announced the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the MA (English) program, under CET Code 113, for the academic session 2026-27. 

Candidates who have qualified the Common Entrance Test (CET) for MA (English) must participate in this offline process to secure their admission.

## Key Details of the Notification

* **Program:** Master of Arts (English)
* **CET Code:** 113
* **Admission Mode:** Offline Counselling (Round 1)
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Notification Date:** June 19, 2026

### What to Do Next?

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates are required to report physically at the designated venue as per the rank-wise schedule. 

For the complete list of reporting dates, times, category-wise rank cutoffs, and required documents, candidates must refer to the official notification PDF published on the university's admission portal. You can also check our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for general preparation tips regarding offline document verification.
