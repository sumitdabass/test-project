## Postponement of Spot Round Allotment

GGSIPU has announced the postponement of the spot round seat allotment results. Candidates registered for this phase must wait for the university to publish the revised notification.

## Important Information for Applicants

Candidates participating in the allotment process are advised to keep track of official announcements. While awaiting updated dates, students can read through the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or learn more about general [GGSIPU counselling](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) procedures.

For verified details and upcoming notices, refer directly to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
