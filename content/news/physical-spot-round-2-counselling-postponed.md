## Physical Spot Round 2 Counselling Delayed

GGSIPU has released an official notification postponing the Physical Spot Round 2 counselling process. This update directly affects applicants seeking seat allotment across multiple undergraduate degree courses.

### Impacted Academic Programmes

The postponement notice covers the following undergraduate offerings:

- Bachelor of Technology (BTech)
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
- Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
- Lateral Entry to BTech (LE-BTech)

### Next Steps for Applicants

Candidates scheduled to attend the offline reporting or physical spot counselling process should wait for the updated timetable before visiting the university campus. 

To understand general allotment steps and university procedures, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). For course-specific updates, review information on [GGSIPU counselling for BTech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) and [IPU BTech admission](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php). Always verify specific dates directly through the official portals at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in.
