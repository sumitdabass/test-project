## GGSIPU Post-Round 1 Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities to be followed after the Round 1 seat allotment. This notification, dated June 30, 2026, applies to multiple undergraduate and professional programs.

### Covered Courses

The schedule details the subsequent admission steps for the following courses:

* Law (LLB)
* Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)
* BA (Journalism & Mass Communication)
* BA (English)
* BA (Economics)
* Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)

### Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates who participated in Round 1 counselling for these programs must log in to the official admission portal to check their allotment status. The subsequent process typically involves:

* **Seat Acceptance Fee Payment:** Paying the required fee to secure the allotted seat.
* **Submit Willingness:** Choosing to 'Freeze' or 'Float' the allotted seat.
* **Withdrawal of Admissions:** Opting out of the counselling process if desired.

For the exact start and end dates of these activities, candidates are advised to refer to the official notification PDF published on the GGSIPU admissions website. You can also consult our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for general insights on the counselling process.
