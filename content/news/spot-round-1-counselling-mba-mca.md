## Spot Round 1 Counselling Notification

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule and guidelines for Spot Round 01 counselling regarding postgraduate admissions for the 2026-27 academic year.

This round caters specifically to eligible applicants seeking remaining seats in professional postgraduate courses. Candidates interested in [MBA admission at IP University](/mba-admission-ip-university.php) or [MCA admission at IPU](/mca-admission-ipu.php) should check the official document for participation rules.

### Key Guidelines for Candidates

* Spot counselling participation requires adherence to specified registration steps and payment of required fees as per university norms.
* All seat allocations depend strictly on merit and seat availability across participating institutes.
* For step-by-step procedures regarding seat allotment, review the overall [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

Applicants are advised to review the full official notification PDF on the official university portal at ipu.ac.in to ensure all submission deadlines are met.
