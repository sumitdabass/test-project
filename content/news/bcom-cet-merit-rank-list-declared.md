## CET 2026 Rank List Issued for B.Com (Code 146)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the official merit list and ranks for candidates who took the Common Entrance Test (CET) 2026 for the Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) program (CET Code 146).

The notification, dated September 16, 2026, details candidate standings for admissions into the 2026–27 academic session.

## Checking Your Position and Next Steps

Applicants can access the detailed rank document on the university admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in. The declared ranks will serve as the basis for subsequent counselling rounds and seat allocations.

Students preparing for upcoming allocation stages can review the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or examine previous trends through the [ipu cet cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) resources for general reference.
