## Round 1 Cutoff Published for Paramedical Courses

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the Round 1 cutoff data for admissions into paramedical bachelor's degree programs under CET Code 124.

### Programs Covered Under Code 124

The cutoff list outlines the opening and closing ranks for several allied health courses:

- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT)
- Bachelor of Prosthetics and Orthotics (BPO)
- Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)
- Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Technology (BSc MLT)
- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (BOT)

## Accessing the Cutoff Details

Applicants can review institute-wise and category-wise closing ranks through the official notification PDF hosted on ipu.admissions.nic.in. For an overview of counselling stages and seat allotment procedures, consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and check previous rank trends through the [IPU CET cutoff guide](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php).
