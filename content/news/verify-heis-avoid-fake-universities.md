## Ensuring Institutional Legitimacy

GGSIPU has released an advisory regarding the verification of Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs). Students are urged to perform due diligence to avoid falling prey to fake universities that operate without proper authorization.

### Verification Steps

Before enrolling in any program, students should verify the institution's credentials through the University Grants Commission (UGC) or other relevant statutory bodies. It is crucial to ensure that the degree offered is recognized and that the institution has the legal right to grant it.

### Safe Admission Practices

Students interested in GGSIPU programs should strictly follow the official [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). For specific courses, such as [MBA admission](/mba-admission-ip-university.php) or [GGSIPU counselling for B-Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php), always use the official university links and verify the seat matrix provided in the official brochures.

### Warning Signs

Be wary of institutions that promise guaranteed placement without academic rigor or those that are not listed on official government portals. When in doubt, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
