## Overview of Round 02 BAMS Notification

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice regarding Round 02 counselling for admission to the Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) program for the 2026–27 academic session.

Admissions under Programme Code 153 are conducted using qualifying ranks from NEET UG 2026.

## Key Program Information

- **Programme Name:** Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS)
- **Programme Code:** 153
- **Admissions Basis:** NEET UG 2026
- **Academic Year:** 2026–27

For a general overview of university admission procedures, applicants can consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Action Required for Candidates

Candidates participating in the Round 02 process should verify all specific deadlines, seat vacancy details, and verification requirements directly from the document released on October 23, 2026. For complete procedural steps, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and the admission portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
