## Cancellation of Offline Spot Round Schedule

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice announcing the withdrawal of the earlier released schedule for the offline spot round of counselling.

The update corresponds to Notification No. 268/2026 under file reference F. No. IPU-7/ DA(Academic /Offline Counselling/2026/2987, originally dated 05.10.2026.

## Impact on Prospective Applicants

Candidates planning to participate in the offline spot round must note that the previous timetable is no longer valid. Anyone tracking seat allotment updates or planning their travel for in-person counselling should halt their arrangements until the university issues a revised notification.

For general procedures and guidance on IP University seat allotments, check the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or review the protocols for technical courses through the [GGSIPU counselling for B Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) overview.

## Next Steps for Students

Applicants should monitor the official admission portals (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in) closely. Any updated schedule, revised seat availability, or rescheduled offline reporting dates will be notified directly by the university administration.
