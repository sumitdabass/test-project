## Overview of M.Ed Offline Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the first round of offline counselling and verification for admission to the Master of Education (M.Ed) program, under CET Code 120, for the academic session 2026-27. 

The notification, dated June 15, 2026, outlines the administrative framework for candidates seeking admission through the offline mode. Prospective students are advised to prepare their documents and fees according to the guidelines specified by the university.

## Key Details and Requirements

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates must physically report to the designated venue as specified in the official university notification. 

### Important Instructions for Candidates

* **Official Schedule:** Candidates must refer to the official notification PDF on the GGSIPU admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in) to verify their specific reporting dates and times based on their CET ranks.
* **Documentation:** Ensure all original academic certificates, CET admit cards, category certificates (if applicable), and required sets of photocopies are ready for verification.
* **Fee Payment:** Candidates should review the official notification for precise details regarding the counselling participation fee and the mode of payment required on the day of counselling.

For a comprehensive understanding of the university's general admission procedures, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
