## New Medical and Para-Medical Courses for 2026-27

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially opened the online application window for several newly introduced medical and para-medical programs. These offerings are part of the academic session for 2026-27.

### Application Details

Prospective students are required to submit their forms through the university's dedicated admission portal. It is recommended that applicants carefully check the specific requirements for these new courses. For general information on the university's entry process, you can consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates should ensure that all necessary documentation is ready for the online submission process. For specific details regarding the selection criteria or entrance requirements for these newly added programs, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
