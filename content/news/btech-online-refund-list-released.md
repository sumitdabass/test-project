## B.Tech Online Refund List Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the online refund list for candidates who applied for fee withdrawal from the B.Tech program (Code 131) for the academic session 2026–27.

The update was issued via Refund Order No. 3949, dated September 25, 2026.

## Checking Your Refund Status

Students who participated in the [GGSIPU counselling for B.Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) and subsequently submitted withdrawal or cancellation requests can review the official order document to verify their details.

- The notification applies strictly to the B.Tech (Code 131) program.
- Candidates can refer to the official portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in to download the full PDF and verify the processed transactions.
- For general admission and counselling policies, review the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
