## GGSIPU Post Basic B.Sc Nursing Merit List Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially published the merit list for admissions to the Post Basic B.Sc (Nursing) program, under course code 188, for the academic session 2026-27. The notification and the list were formally released on June 25, 2026.

Candidates who applied for this program can now access the merit list to verify their rank and qualification status for the upcoming admission rounds.

## How to Access the Merit List

The university has uploaded the complete merit list in PDF format on its official admissions portal. Prospective students can download the document directly to check their names, roll numbers, and assigned ranks. 

For detailed information on the subsequent counselling schedule and registration steps, candidates should consult the [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or refer to the official university website at ipu.ac.in.
