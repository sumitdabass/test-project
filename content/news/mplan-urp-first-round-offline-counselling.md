## M.Plan (URP) Code 368 Offline Counselling Details

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the schedule for the first round of offline counselling and verification for admission to the Master of Planning (Urban and Regional Planning) program, CET Code 368, for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates seeking admission to this postgraduate program must note that the process will be conducted entirely in offline mode. 

## Key Requirements for Candidates

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates must prepare the necessary documents and adhere to the guidelines set by the university:

* **Physical Reporting:** Candidates are required to report physically to the designated venue according to their rank and category slot.
* **Document Verification:** You must carry all original academic documents, certificates, and identity proofs along with self-attested photocopies.
* **Fee Payment:** Keep the prescribed admission fee ready as specified in the official university notification.

For general information on GGSIPU admission procedures, you can check our comprehensive [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## How to Access the Detailed Schedule

The specific rank-wise reporting dates, times, and venue details are listed in the official PDF document. Candidates are advised to download the official notification directly from the university's admissions portal to ensure they do not miss their designated slot.
