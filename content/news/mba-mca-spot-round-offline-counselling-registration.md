## Overview of Spot Round Registration

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has released an official notice inviting online registration for the spot round of offline counselling for [MBA admissions](/mba-admission-ip-university.php) and [MCA admissions](/mca-admission-ipu.php) for the 2026–27 academic year.

### Eligibility Conditions

* **Target Applicants:** This opportunity is strictly reserved for candidates who are currently not admitted to the respective programs.
* **Exclusions:** Candidates who have already been admitted during previous allotment rounds are not eligible to participate.

## Summary of Details

* **Programs:** MBA and MCA
* **Registration Mode:** Online
* **Counselling Mode:** Offline
* **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026

Eligible candidates should complete the online registration form through the official portal and verify specific venue and timing details in the official notification document available on the university admissions website.
