## Stray Round Counselling for MBBS (Code 103)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification regarding the stray vacancy round for MBBS admissions for the academic session 2026-27. 

Candidates participating in the admission process under CET Code 103 must hold valid NEET UG 2026 qualifications to be considered for seat allotment.

## Important Information for Candidates

### Eligibility and Participation
Admission to the MBBS programme at GGSIPU affiliated medical colleges is conducted based on NEET UG merit. Candidates interested in the stray vacancy round should review the official guidelines released by the university.

For broader insights into general university admission procedures, consult the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Official Documentation
Details regarding reporting procedures, remaining seat availability, and specific instructions for the stray round have been outlined in the university's notification dated 22.10.2026. For the complete document and schedule instructions, candidates should refer to the official portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
