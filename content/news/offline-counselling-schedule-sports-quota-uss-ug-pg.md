## Sports Quota Counselling for UG and PG Programmes

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice detailing the offline counselling schedule for reserved sports quota seats. This allotment round applies to both undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) programmes for the 2026–27 academic session.

The allocation covers programmes conducted at the University Schools of Studies (USS) as well as the university's designated Centres of Excellence.

## Key Highlights of the Notification

- **Admission Category:** Sports Quota seat reservation.
- **Programmes Covered:** Undergraduate and Postgraduate courses.
- **Participating Campuses:** University Schools of Studies (USS) and Centres of Excellence.
- **Mode of Allotment:** In-person / offline counselling.

Prospective candidates should review general admission requirements in the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) to ensure all prerequisite criteria are met before attending offline rounds.

## Attending Offline Counselling

Eligible applicants seeking admission under the sports category must attend the counselling session in person as outlined in the university notice. Candidates should prepare and carry all original sports certificates, academic transcripts, verification forms, and identification documents required for physical verification.

For exact reporting times, reporting venue, and candidate rank lists, refer to the official document hosted at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
