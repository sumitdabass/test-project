## BBA Spot Round 02 Vacancies Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the tentative vacant seat details for the second online spot round of counselling for the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme (CET Code 125) for the academic session 2026-27.

The seat status reflects the vacancies available across participating institutes as of August 14, 2026, at 04:00 PM.

## Key Details for Candidates

- **Programme:** BBA (Code 125)
- **Round:** Spot Round 02 (Online)
- **Academic Session:** 2026–27
- **Status Reference Date:** August 14, 2026 (04:00 PM)

Candidates participating in the second spot counselling round should review the detailed institute-wise and category-wise seat distribution before submitting or modifying their choices. For broader admissions procedures and background details, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and check previous trends via [IPU CET cutoff details](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php).

## How to Access the Seat Matrix

Candidates can view the complete PDF notification on the official IPU admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in) to verify remaining open, reserved, and institutional seats for the online spot round.
