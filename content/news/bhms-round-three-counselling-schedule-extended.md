## Overview of the Revised BHMS Schedule

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice detailing an extended timeline for post-result activities in Round 03 of counselling for the Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS, Code 154) programme for the 2026–27 academic session.

The revised timeline comes following the declaration of the Round 03 seat allotment results. Candidates seeking admission under NEET UG 2026 merit should check the official admission portal to ensure all necessary steps are completed according to the updated schedule.

## Actions for Allotted Candidates

Following the outcome of Round 03 allotments, participating students must follow the procedures set out by the university, which typically include:

- Checking individual seat allotment status on the portal.
- Completing document verification and fee payment within the updated deadlines.
- Reporting to the allotted institute where applicable.

For broader procedural context on university counselling and reporting workflows, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Information

Candidates must consult the circular dated 02.11.2026 on the official admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in to verify the exact cutoff dates and deadlines for all Round 03 BHMS activities.
