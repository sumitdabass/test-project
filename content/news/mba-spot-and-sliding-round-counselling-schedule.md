## MBA Spot and Sliding Round Notification

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official schedule for the online spot round counselling, which will be followed by a sliding round, for admissions into the MBA programme (Code 101) for the 2026–27 academic session.

Prospective management students participating in the ongoing admission process can consult the official document for specific round timelines and activity windows.

## Overview of the Notification

- **Programme:** MBA (Code 101)
- **Counselling Stages:** Online Spot Round followed by Sliding Round
- **Academic Session:** 2026–27

For broader context on eligibility rules and the overall admission framework, check our [IPU MBA admission overview](/mba-admission-ip-university.php) alongside the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Next Steps for Candidates

1. Visit the official university admissions website at ipu.admissions.nic.in to review the complete schedule.
2. Verify the prescribed deadlines for spot round registration, choice filling, and seat acceptance.
3. Keep required documentation and applicable counselling fees prepared according to university instructions.
