## New Medical and Para-Medical Programs Introduced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the introduction of new medical and para-medical programs starting from the 2026-27 academic session. The university is now inviting online application forms from eligible candidates for these specific courses.

## Online Application Process

Prospective students can access the application portal via the official university website. It is mandatory for all applicants to complete the registration and form submission online. For those unfamiliar with the general requirements of the university, reviewing the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) is recommended before starting the process.

## Important Guidelines

Candidates should ensure they meet the specific eligibility criteria for the newly introduced programs as detailed in the official notification. All necessary documentation should be scanned and ready for upload during the online submission process. For further details on entrance examinations or merit-based criteria, students should refer to the official university notification at ipu.ac.in.
