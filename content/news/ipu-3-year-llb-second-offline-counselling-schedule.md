## Second Offline Counselling for 3-Year LLB (Code 238)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the second round of offline counselling for admissions to the 3-Year LLB program (Code 238) for the academic session 2026-27. The notification was formally issued on July 17, 2026.

Candidates who are participating in this round must carefully review the schedule to identify their specific reporting dates and times based on their ranks and categories.

## Important Instructions for Candidates

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates are required to physically report to the designated venue as specified by the university. 

* **Verification of Details:** Candidates must check the official notification PDF on the university's admission portal to verify their rank-wise slot.
* **Required Documents:** Ensure all necessary academic documents, category certificates (if applicable), and demand drafts/fees are prepared according to the guidelines in the official notice.
* **Admissions Guide:** For a general overview of how the university conducts its admission processes, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For the exact schedule details, seat matrix, and reporting venue, please refer to the official PDF notification on the IPU admissions website.
