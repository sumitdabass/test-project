## M.Ed. Admission Notice Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification regarding admissions to the Master of Education (M.Ed.) programme through CUET PG.

### Key Details for Applicants

Prospective students applying for the M.Ed. course should inspect the notification released on July 31, 2026, on the official portal. To learn more about general entry procedures, review the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For precise schedule updates and candidate guidelines, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
