## First Round Offline Counselling Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the MPO (CET Code 109) program. This schedule applies to admissions for the academic session 2026-27. The official notification was published on June 25, 2026.

## Key Details and Next Steps

Candidates who qualified for the CET Code 109 exam must participate in this offline round to secure their seat allocation. Since specific reporting dates, times, and venue instructions are detailed in the official PDF, candidates are advised to review the official notification directly.

To understand the general procedure for university admissions, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Instructions for Candidates

* **Document Verification:** Ensure you have all required academic documents, certificates, and CET admit cards ready before attending the venue.
* **Fees and Drafts:** Check the official notification for details regarding the required counselling participation fees or demand drafts.
* **Official Updates:** Always refer to the official university website at ipu.ac.in for any sudden changes or updates to the offline schedule.
