## IPU B.MRIT First Counselling Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the first round of counselling for the Bachelor of Medical Radiology & Imaging Technology (B.MRIT) program. 

Please note that the program, previously known as B.Sc. (MIT), has undergone a change in nomenclature and is now officially designated as **B.MRIT** under **CET Code 134**.

## Key Details of the Notification

* **Program Name:** Bachelor of Medical Radiology & Imaging Technology (B.MRIT) — formerly B.Sc. (MIT)
* **CET Code:** 134
* **Notification Date:** June 23, 2026
* **Purpose:** First round of counselling schedule

Candidates who qualified in the Common Entrance Test (CET) for Code 134 must review the official schedule to participate in the upcoming counselling rounds.

## Next Steps for Candidates

Since the schedule details are subject to specific rank brackets and categories, candidates are advised to download the official PDF from the IPU admissions portal to verify their specific reporting dates, times, and venue details. 

Ensure you have all required documents, including your CET admit card, rank card, academic certificates, and category certificates (if applicable), ready before the counselling session begins. For a comprehensive overview of the university's admission procedures, check our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
