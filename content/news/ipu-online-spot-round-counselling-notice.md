## GGSIPU Announces Online Spot Round Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification regarding the online spot round of counselling for the 2026–27 academic year.

This round is conducted for candidates seeking admission across various undergraduate and postgraduate programs offered by the university and its affiliated institutes.

### Courses Included in the Spot Round

The notification applies to the following programs:

* **Engineering:** B.Tech and Lateral Entry to B.Tech. Learn more about the process via our [GGSIPU B.Tech counselling guide](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php).
* **Law:** LLM, Integrated BA-LLB, and Integrated BBA-LLB.
* **Management & Commerce:** BBA, 5-year Integrated BBA-MBA, and B.Com (Honours).
* **Computer Applications:** BCA.
* **Education & Humanities:** B.Ed. and BA (Economics).

## Next Steps for Applicants

Prospective students who wish to participate in the spot allocation round must check the official portal for detailed eligibility criteria, participation fees, and submission deadlines. For general admission procedures, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

All official instructions and fee payment details are available in the notification published on ipu.ac.in.
