## B.Pharm (Code 133) Second Offline Counselling Details

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially announced the schedule for the second round of offline counselling for the Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) program, under subject code 133, for the academic session 2026-27. 

This round of counselling is being conducted based on the merit secured by candidates in the IPU Common Entrance Test (CET) 2026.

## Key Instructions for Candidates

As this is an offline counselling process, registered candidates must physically report to the designated venue as specified by the university. 

* **Admission Criteria:** Admissions will be strictly allocated based on the CET 2026 merit rank.
* **Document Verification:** Candidates must carry all original documents, academic certificates, category certificates (if applicable), and the requisite fees for verification and seat allotment.
* **Official Schedule:** For the exact date, time, and venue details corresponding to specific merit ranks, candidates are advised to refer directly to the official notification PDF published on the university's admission portal.

For comprehensive information on navigating the university's admission cycles, you can consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
