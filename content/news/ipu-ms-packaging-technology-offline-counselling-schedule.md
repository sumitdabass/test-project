## First Round Offline Counselling for MS (Packaging Technology)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially announced the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the MS (Packaging Technology) program, CET Code 604, for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who have qualified for this program must participate in the offline verification and seat allotment process according to the university's designated timeline.

## Key Information for Candidates

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates are required to report physically to the specified venue. Ensure you have all necessary documents ready for verification.

### What to Prepare
*   **Documents:** Keep your academic certificates, CET admit card, rank card, and category certificates (if applicable) ready.
*   **Fees:** Be prepared with the requisite counselling and admission fees as prescribed in the official notification.
*   **Official Notification:** For exact reporting dates, times, and venue details, candidates must refer directly to the official PDF notification released on the GGSIPU admissions portal.

For more general information on how the university conducts its admission processes, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
