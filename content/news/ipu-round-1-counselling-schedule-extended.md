## Extended Round 1 Counselling Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding the extension of the online counselling schedule for Round 1. This extension applies to the specific academic programmes previously detailed in the university's Notification No. 34/2026, which was published on June 10, 2026.

Candidates participating in the current admission cycle should review the updated timeline to ensure they complete their online counselling activities within the newly extended window. For general guidance on navigating the process, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Important Exclusions

Please note that this schedule extension does not apply to all courses. Specifically, **Code 128: Lateral Entry (LE) B.Tech for Diploma Holders** is excluded from this notification. Candidates seeking admission to other engineering streams can keep track of updates via the [GGSIPU B.Tech Counselling Guide](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php).

### Next Steps for Candidates

To avoid missing critical deadlines for choice filling, registration, or document verification, candidates are advised to:
1. Visit the official IPU admissions portal at `ipu.admissions.nic.in`.
2. Download the detailed PDF notification dated 17.06.2026 to verify the exact extended dates for their respective program codes.
3. Complete all pending online counselling steps before the revised closing dates.
