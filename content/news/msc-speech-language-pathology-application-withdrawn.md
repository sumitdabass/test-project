## Application Process Suspended for M.Sc. SLP (Code 168)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification announcing the withdrawal of the online application form submission process for the M.Sc. Speech Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP) program. This specific course is registered under GGSIPU course code 168.

The decision was formalized through an official university notice dated May 19, 2026.

### What This Means for Prospective Students

Candidates interested in pursuing the M.Sc. SLP program at GGSIPU should note that the online portal will no longer accept new application submissions for this course code. 

If you have already submitted an application or require details regarding the next steps, fee refunds, or alternative options, please consult the official university website at ipu.ac.in or contact the GGSIPU admission helpdesk. For information regarding other active courses and general registration procedures, you can explore our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
