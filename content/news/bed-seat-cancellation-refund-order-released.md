## B.Ed Seat Cancellation Refund Order

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official refund notice for candidates enrolled in the Bachelor of Education (B.Ed, CET Code 122) program for the academic session 2026-27.

The directive, released under Order No. 3946 dated September 24, 2026, concerns candidates whose allotted seats were subsequently cancelled.

### Accessing the Refund Details

Candidates who requested cancellations or had their admissions cancelled during the ongoing admission cycle should review the circular on the official portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in). The document outlines the specific list of candidates eligible for fee refunds.

For broader procedures regarding admissions and cancellations, review the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
