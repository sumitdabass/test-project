## Overview of B.Pharm Offline Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the official schedule for the first round of offline counselling and physical admissions for the Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) program, designated under CET Code 133, for the academic session 2026-27. 

This notification, released on July 8, 2026, is critical for all qualified candidates seeking admission into the B.Pharm program. For a broader understanding of university admission policies, candidates can consult our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Key Requirements for Candidates

Because this process is conducted offline, candidates must physically report to the designated university venue to secure their seats. 

### Essential Checklist for Offline Reporting
* **Rank-Wise Schedule:** Candidates must report strictly according to the date and time slot assigned to their respective ranks in the official PDF.
* **Original Documents:** You must carry all original academic certificates, mark sheets, character certificates, and category certificates (if applicable) for physical verification.
* **Admission Fee:** Candidates must be prepared to pay the required fee in the format specified by the university in the official notification.

Candidates are strongly advised to download the official PDF from the GGSIPU admissions portal to verify their exact reporting schedule and ensure they do not miss their designated slot.
