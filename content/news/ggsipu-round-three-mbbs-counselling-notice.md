## MBBS Round 3 Counselling Update

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice regarding Round 03 of counselling for admission to the MBBS program (Program Code 103) for the 2026–27 academic session. The admissions are being conducted on the basis of NEET UG 2026 scores.

### Key Details

- **Program:** MBBS (Code 103)
- **Admission Basis:** NEET UG 2026
- **Counselling Phase:** Round 03
- **Academic Year:** 2026–27
- **Notice Date:** October 1, 2026

### Instructions for Candidates

Students participating in this phase must consult the uploaded notification document directly to verify mandatory procedures, seat allotments, reporting schedules, and document verification rules. For general guidance on university entry processes, consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For verified schedules, eligibility criteria, and fee deposit instructions, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in or ipu.admissions.nic.in.
