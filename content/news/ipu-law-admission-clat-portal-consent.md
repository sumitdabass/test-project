## Consent Required on NLU Consortium Portal

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification for candidates seeking admission to its undergraduate and postgraduate law programs for the 2026 academic session. 

Candidates who have qualified or appeared for the CLAT UG 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 examinations must provide their consent on the official portal of the Consortium of NLUs to be considered for the GGSIPU admission process.

## Affected Law Programs

This mandatory consent process applies to the following law programs at GGSIPU:

*   **Integrated BA-LLB / BBA-LLB** (Program Code 121) via CLAT UG 2026
*   **Master of Laws (LLM)** (Program Code 112) via CLAT PG 2026

## Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates must log in to the Consortium of NLUs portal to submit their consent as per the university's guidelines. For step-by-step instructions on the overall university registration process, candidates can refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For specific deadlines and the official PDF notification, please visit the official IPU admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
