## Consent Required on NLU Consortium Portal

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding admissions to its integrated law and postgraduate law programmes. Candidates who have appeared for CLAT UG 2026 and CLAT PG 2026 must submit their consent on the official portal of the Consortium of NLUs to be considered for the university's admission process.

This requirement applies to the following programmes:
* **Integrated BA-LLB / BBA-LLB** (Code 121) via CLAT UG 2026
* **Master of Laws (LLM)** (Code 112) via CLAT PG 2026

### Key Instructions for Candidates

Candidates seeking admission to these law courses at GGSIPU must ensure they complete the consent process on the Consortium of NLUs portal. Failing to provide consent may affect eligibility for the subsequent admission and counselling rounds.

For comprehensive details on the step-by-step registration process, eligibility criteria, and seat allotment, candidates are advised to consult the [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and monitor the official university website at ipu.ac.in.
