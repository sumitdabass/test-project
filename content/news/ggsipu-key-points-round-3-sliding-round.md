## Guidelines for Round 3 and Sliding Round

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published essential guidelines for candidates participating in Round 3 and the upcoming Sliding Round of counselling.

Candidates applying for technical, professional, and humanities programs should carefully read the official notice before proceeding with their choice filling or seat acceptance steps. For general preparation and process workflows, consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Covered Programs

This counselling update strictly applies to the following degree programs:

* **Law:** LLM, 5-Year LLB
* **Computer Applications & Management:** BCA, BBA / BBA-MBA Integrated
* **Engineering:** Lateral Entry B.Tech (For standard B.Tech updates, review [GGSIPU B.Tech counselling information](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php))
* **Humanities & Media:** BA (JMC), BA (English), BA (Economics)
* **Education & Commerce:** B.Ed, B.Com

## Key Recommendations for Candidates

Applicants involved in these rounds are advised to check their allotment status on the official admissions portal. For specific details on timelines, fee payments, and reporting requirements, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
