## Application Process Halted for M.Sc. SLP (Code 168)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially announced the withdrawal of the online application form submission for the **M.Sc. Speech Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP)** program. This postgraduate program is registered under **Code 168** for the current academic session.

The official notification regarding this withdrawal was released by the university administration on May 19, 2026.

### Key Information for Applicants

If you were planning to apply for this course, please keep the following points in mind:

* **Suspension of Submissions:** The online portal will no longer accept new application submissions for the M.Sc. SLP program under Code 168.
* **Further Clarifications:** Since specific reasons or subsequent schedules have not been detailed in the brief notice, candidates must refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in for clarification on registration status or next steps.

For general information on how to navigate the university's entry requirements, you can read our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
