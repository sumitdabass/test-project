## Overview of B.Sc Yoga Seat Status

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the official seat status for the B.Sc (Yoga) program (CET Code 187) for the academic session 2026-27. This update comes immediately after the conclusion of the first round of offline counselling held on July 11, 2026.

## Key Details for Candidates

Candidates who participated in the initial round or are planning to participate in subsequent rounds can now check the remaining vacant seats across participating institutes. 

* **Course Name:** B.Sc (Yoga)
* **CET Code:** 187
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Status Date:** July 11, 2026

To plan your next steps in the admission process, you can consult our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps

Prospective students are advised to review the official vacant seat matrix to understand their chances in the upcoming counselling rounds. For the exact category-wise distribution of the remaining seats, please refer to the official notification available on the university's admission portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in).
