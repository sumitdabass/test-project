## Round 2 Counselling Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official notification detailing the schedule for the second round of counselling for admission to the Bachelor of Clinical Psychology programme for the 2026–27 academic session.

## Important Programme Details

- **Course:** Bachelor of Clinical Psychology
- **CET Code:** 163
- **Academic Session:** 2026–27
- **Notification Date:** August 17, 2026

Students who qualified under CET Code 163 and are eligible for the second round should consult the university schedule for their designated reporting timelines and procedures. For broader context on how university admissions are structured, visit the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Actions for Candidates

Eligible applicants should visit the official admissions portal at `ipu.admissions.nic.in` to download the notification PDF, verify their specific dates, and prepare all required documentation prior to participating in the counselling round.
