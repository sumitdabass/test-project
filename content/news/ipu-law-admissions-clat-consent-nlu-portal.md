## Consent Required on NLU Portal for IPU Law Admissions

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an important notice for candidates seeking admission to its undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes for the academic session 2026-27. 

Candidates who have qualified or appeared for CLAT UG 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 must provide their consent on the official portal of the Consortium of NLUs to be considered for the GGSIPU admission process. 

This requirement applies directly to the following programmes:
* **Integrated BA-LLB / BBA-LLB** (Code 121)
* **Master of Laws (LLM)** (Code 112)

## Key Steps for Candidates

To ensure your candidacy is processed for GGSIPU law admissions, follow these steps:
1. Visit the official portal of the Consortium of NLUs.
2. Log in using your credentials.
3. Locate the consent option for sharing your CLAT 2026 scores with Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University and submit your agreement.

For a comprehensive overview of the university's registration and counselling steps, you can refer to our detailed [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Guidelines

* **Data Consistency:** Ensure that all personal details, test roll numbers, and scores match exactly across both the NLU portal and the GGSIPU application portal.
* **Official Verification:** For specific dates, deadlines, and detailed procedures, candidates are advised to refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and monitor the portal regularly.
