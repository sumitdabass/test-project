## Postponement of Offline Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notice regarding the postponement of the offline schedule for the extended open house counselling round for B.Sc (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics), course code 137.

This update applies to candidates seeking admission for the 2025–2026 academic session.

## Next Steps for Applicants

Candidates who were planning to attend the offline open house session should monitor university portals for revised schedules or further instructions. For broader information on university entry processes, consult our [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

Detailed official updates and any revised dates will be published on the official admission portal at ipu.ac.in.
