## Overview of BA Economics Refund Notice

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official fee refund notice for candidates admitted to the BA (Economics) programme (Code 197) for the academic session 2026–27.

The directive, documented under Refund Order No. 3956 and dated September 30, 2026, concerns applicants who did not report to their allotted institutes during the admissions cycle. For more guidance on university admission policies, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Details of Refund Order No. 3956

### Affected Candidates

This order applies exclusively to candidates who were allocated seats in BA (Economics) but did not complete the reporting procedure at their respective colleges.

### Verification and Next Steps

- The notification lists details of eligible candidates scheduled to receive a refund under university regulations.
- Candidates are advised to cross-check their names and application credentials directly in the official notification.
- For any specific issues or clarifications regarding account credits, students should consult the official IPU admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
