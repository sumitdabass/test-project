## Round 2 Schedule for BAMS Admissions

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice detailing the schedule of post-result activities for Round 02 of BAMS (Code 153) admissions for NEET UG qualified candidates.

The document outlines the mandatory follow-up steps for applicants participating in the ongoing counselling process for the academic session. Candidates seeking general information on university admission protocols can consult the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Actions for Candidates

Applicants allotted seats or participating in Round 02 counselling should complete all scheduled activities within the stipulated timeframes.

### Next Steps

- Visit the official admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in to review the complete schedule.
- Download and read the official notification dated 28.10.2026 for instructions regarding seat confirmation, fee payment, and document verification.
- Adhere strictly to the designated university deadlines to avoid cancellation of allotment.
