## Official Advisory Issued by GGSIPU

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification warning prospective students and their parents about fraudulent individuals and touts. These unauthorized agents falsely claim to guarantee seats or secure direct admissions for the academic session 2026-27 in exchange for money.

The university has clarified that all admissions are strictly merit-based and must go through the official channels. To understand the legitimate steps required to secure your seat, please refer to our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Key Guidelines for Safe Admission

To protect yourself from admission scams, keep the following points in mind:

* **No Direct Admissions:** GGSIPU does not offer direct admissions through external agencies. Any offer of a guaranteed seat outside the official merit list is fraudulent.
* **Official Portals Only:** All registration, fee payments, and choice filling must be done exclusively through the official GGSIPU portals (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in).
* **Merit-Based Process:** Seats are allocated solely based on performance in the respective entrance exams and subsequent counselling rounds. You can check past trends in our guide on [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php).

## Reporting Fraudulent Activities

If you are approached by any individual or agency claiming to have an "admission quota" or promising a seat through unfair means, you are advised to report the matter immediately to the university administration or local law enforcement authorities. Relying on unauthorized agents can lead to financial loss and the cancellation of your candidature.
