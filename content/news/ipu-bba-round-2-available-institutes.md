## GGSIPU Releases BBA College List for Round 2 Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially published the list of BBA institutes participating in Round 2 of the online counselling process for the 2026-27 academic session. The notification, dated July 14, 2026, serves as an essential resource for candidates preparing to lock or modify their choices for the upcoming counselling round.

## How to Use the Institute List

Candidates participating in the second round of counselling should carefully review this list to verify which colleges are offering seats. To ensure a strategic choice-filling process, students can consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for general advice on navigating the university's online counselling portal.

## Important Details

* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Course:** Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
* **Counselling Phase:** Round 02
* **Release Date of Notice:** July 14, 2026

Candidates are advised to cross-reference this list with previous cutoff trends to make informed decisions. For the exact seat matrix, specific college codes, and the official PDF document, please refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
