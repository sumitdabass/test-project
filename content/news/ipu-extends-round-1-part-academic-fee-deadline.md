## Round 1 Fee Payment Deadline Extended

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification extending the last date for submitting the Part Academic Fee for Round 1. This extension applies to multiple undergraduate and postgraduate programs for the academic session 2026-27.

## Eligible Programmes

The extension for the Round 1 Part Academic Fee submission is applicable to the following courses:

* LLM
* BCA
* B.Tech (refer to the [GGSIPU B.Tech Counselling guide](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) for process details)
* BBA & Allied Programmes
* 5-Year Integrated BBA-MBA

## Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates who have been allotted seats in Round 1 but have not yet paid their fees must complete the transaction online through the official admission portal. For the exact extended date and time, candidates should refer to the official PDF notification published on the IPU admissions website. For general assistance on the process, check the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
