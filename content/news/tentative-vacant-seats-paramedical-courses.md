## Vacancy Details for Paramedical Programmes

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the tentative vacant seat matrix for admissions to various undergraduate paramedical programs for the 2026–27 academic session.

The released list covers vacancies across the following degrees:

- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.PT)
- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (B.OT)
- Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)
- Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Sciences (BMLS)
- Bachelor of Prosthetics and Orthotics (BPO)

These programs correspond to CET codes 119 and 124.

## Next Steps for Candidates

Applicants seeking seat allocation in subsequent counselling rounds should refer to the officially published document to assess category-wise and institute-wise seat availability. To understand general procedures and preparation for university allotments, explore the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For exact figures and further scheduling instructions, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in.
