## Deadline Extension for D.Pharm and M.Pharm Admissions

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding the extension of the last date for online application form submissions for D.Pharm and M.Pharm courses.

### Overview of the Announcement

* **Eligible Courses:** Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) and Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm).
* **Notice Date:** August 5, 2026.
* **Next Steps:** Candidates who missed the earlier submission window can complete their registration on the official portal.

For general steps on registration and eligibility, candidates can consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). For specific revised dates and detailed instructions, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
