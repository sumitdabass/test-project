## Special Offline Counselling Notice

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice regarding a special round of offline counselling for multiple undergraduate programs for the 2026–27 academic session.

### Included Programs
This offline counselling session applies to applicants targeting the following undergraduate courses:
- Bachelor of Hotel Management and Catering Technology (BHMCT)
- B.Sc (Yoga)
- Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS)
- BS (Packaging Technology)

### Entrance Exam Criteria
Candidates are eligible to participate based on their performance in:
- National Level Tests (NLT)
- IPU Common Entrance Test (CET)
- Common University Entrance Test (CUET)

Candidates should refer to the official notification on ipu.ac.in for detailed reporting instructions and schedule updates. For broader details on university procedure, consult our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
