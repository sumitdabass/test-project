## Spot Round 2 Reporting and Fee Payment Overview

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the official schedule for fee payment and reporting for online Spot Round 2 counselling for the academic session 2026-27.

This round covers candidates seeking admission through National Level Tests (NLT) and the university's Common Entrance Test (CET).

### Eligible Programmes

The updated schedule applies to the following undergraduate programmes:

- Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
- Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
- Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) [B.Com (H)]
- Integrated Law Programmes (LLB)

Students participating in engineering admissions can refer to the [GGSIPU counselling for B-Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) overview or check general procedures in the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Steps for Allotted Candidates

1. **Fee Payment**: Candidates allotted seats in Spot Round 2 must complete the requisite fee payment within the prescribed timeframe announced by the university.
2. **Document Verification & Reporting**: Shortlisted applicants must complete their reporting procedures as stipulated in the notification.
3. **Official Notification**: Complete date-wise instructions, reporting timelines, and list of required documents are available in the official notification on ipu.admissions.nic.in.
