## Advisory on Document Validity

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice regarding the validity of academic marksheets and category certificates submitted by applicants for the 2026-27 academic session.

## Categories and Certificates Affected

The communication covers verification rules for multiple reserved categories and academic records, including:

- Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST)
- Other Backward Classes (OBC)
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS)
- Defence quota and Persons with Disabilities (PwD)
- Academic marksheets

Applicants undergoing document verification should ensure their certificates meet all eligibility and validity norms. For detailed steps on navigating university entry requirements, consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Applicants

Candidates must ensure all uploaded document copies and reservation certificates are current and issued by authorized authorities. For precise details regarding certificate formats and compliance, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
