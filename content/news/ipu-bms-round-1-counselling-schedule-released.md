## IPU BMS Counselling Schedule Details

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the first round of counselling for the Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) program, under program code 142, for the academic session 2026-27. The notification was published on June 29, 2026.

Candidates who have qualified for the BMS admissions process must review the official schedule to understand their specific reporting dates, document verification timelines, and venue details.

## Key Steps for Candidates

To participate in the upcoming counselling round, candidates should prepare the following:

*   **Verify Rank-Wise Dates:** Check the official PDF to identify the exact date and time slot allocated to your rank.
*   **Document Preparation:** Keep all academic certificates, category certificates (if applicable), and admit cards ready for verification.
*   **Counselling Venue:** Confirm the designated venue or online procedure specified in the official notification.

For general guidance on navigating the university's admission process, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Official Notification Link

Candidates are advised to download and read the complete schedule directly from the official portal: [IPU Admissions Current Events](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ea119a40c1592979f51819b0bd38d39d/uploads/2026/06/202606291762689946.pdf).
