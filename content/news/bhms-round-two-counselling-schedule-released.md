## Round 2 BHMS Counselling Schedule

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official schedule outlining subsequent procedures following the Round 02 seat allocation for the Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS, Programme Code 154) course for the 2026–27 academic year.

All allocations for this programme are based strictly on candidate performance in NEET UG. Candidates who participated in this round should review the official timeline to complete post-allotment formalities.

## Next Steps for Allotted Candidates

### Document Verification and Fee Submission

Candidates who have been allotted seats in Round 02 must adhere to the prescribed university timeline for completing all post-result requirements. For general admission policies and procedural norms, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Official Verification

Specific deadlines for seat acceptance, fee payments, and reporting requirements are listed in the official university circular. Candidates must refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in to verify the exact cutoff dates and avoid forfeiture of allotted seats.
