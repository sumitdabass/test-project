## Application Deadline Extended for New Healthcare Programmes

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding the extension of the last date for submitting online application forms. This extension is applicable to the newly introduced medical and paramedical programmes for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who are planning to apply for these newly launched courses now have additional time to complete their registration process online.

## Eligible Programmes

This extension is strictly for:
* Newly introduced Paramedical programmes
* Newly introduced Medical programmes

To understand the general registration steps and requirements, you can read our comprehensive [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Applicants

Since the exact extended date is not detailed in the initial headline release, candidates are strongly advised to download the official notification PDF dated 01.06.2026 from the IPU admissions portal to verify the final submission deadline. Ensure all required documents and application fees are submitted online before the revised cutoff date.
