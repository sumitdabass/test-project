## B.Com (Hons) Spot Round 2 Vacancies Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the tentative vacant seat status for the second online spot round of counselling for the B.Com (Hons) programme (CET Code 146) for the 2026–27 academic session.

The seat status reflects the vacancies available as of August 14, 2026 (4:00 PM). Candidates interested in securing a seat in affiliated institutes can review the distribution of vacant seats across categories before proceeding with the spot counselling process. For general guidance on university processes, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Details for Applicants

- **Programme:** B.Com (Hons) (Programme Code 146)
- **Counselling Round:** Online Spot Round 2
- **Seat Status Cutoff:** Updated as on August 14, 2026 (by 4:00 PM)
- **Official Notification:** Available on the IPU admission website (ipu.admissions.nic.in)

Candidates are advised to review the official seat matrix directly from the university admission portal before finalizing their participation and college preferences.
