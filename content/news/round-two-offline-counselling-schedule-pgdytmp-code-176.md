## Round 2 Offline Counselling for PGDYTMP

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice detailing the schedule for the second round of offline counselling for the Post Graduate Diploma in Yoga Therapy for Medical Professionals (PGDYTMP, CET Code 176) for the 2026–27 academic session.

Candidates participating in this round should review the official notification released on October 8, 2026, to verify reporting slots, seat availability, and venue instructions.

## Key Information for Candidates

- **Program**: Post Graduate Diploma in Yoga Therapy for Medical Professionals (PGDYTMP)
- **CET Code**: 176
- **Round**: 2nd Round
- **Mode**: Offline Counselling
- **Academic Session**: 2026–27

For broader procedures regarding university admissions and seat allotments, check the general [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps

Eligible applicants must review the complete document and procedural guidelines directly in the official notification available at ipu.admissions.nic.in before attending the offline counselling session.
