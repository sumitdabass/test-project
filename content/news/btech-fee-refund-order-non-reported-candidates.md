## B.Tech Fee Refund Notice Issued

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official refund order (Order No. 3970) for candidates who participated in the B.Tech (CET Code 131) admission process for the 2026–27 academic year.

The directive pertains to candidates who secured allotment during the seat allocation process but were marked as "not reported" in the online counselling system.

## Details of Refund Order No. 3970

According to the official circular published on the admission portal:

* **Program:** B.Tech (Code 131)
* **Target Group:** Online non-reported candidates
* **Academic Session:** 2026–27
* **Order Reference:** Order No. 3970 (dated 05.10.2026)

Students who withdrew or did not complete their reporting procedure during [GGSIPU counselling for B.Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) should inspect the document to ensure their names and account details are listed correctly.

## Next Steps for Affected Candidates

Candidates included under this order are advised to verify their application records against the published list. For further instructions regarding transaction statuses or individual candidate details, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in or consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for standard university processes.
