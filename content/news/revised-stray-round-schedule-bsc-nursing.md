## Revised Stray Round Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an updated counselling notification outlining the revised stray round schedule for admission to B.Sc (Hons) Nursing (Program Code 115) for the 2026–27 session.

The revised schedule pertains to eligible applicants who have qualified via NEET UG 2026 as well as candidates who are CET qualified for the 2026 intake.

## Key Highlights of the Notification

- **Course:** B.Sc (Hons) Nursing (CET Code 115)
- **Round:** Stray Vacancy Round
- **Target Candidates:** NEET UG 2026 qualified and CET qualified candidates
- **Academic Year:** 2026–27

Students looking for broader information on university admission rules and general procedures can consult the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Instructions for Candidates

Because stray round activities follow strict timelines, all eligible applicants must refer directly to the official notification document on ipu.admissions.nic.in or ipu.ac.in for venue details, exact cutoffs, seat matrices, and registration deadlines.
