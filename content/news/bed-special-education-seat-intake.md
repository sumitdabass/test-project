## Overview of B.Ed. Special Education Seat Intake

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the sanctioned seat intake for the B.Ed. (Special Education) programme (Code 159) for the 2026-27 academic session.

## Seat Matrix and Management Quota

The released document specifies the total approved seat capacity across affiliated institutes offering the programme, including allocations reserved for the Management Quota. For broader details on university admission norms, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Information for Applicants

Candidates participating in the admission process should review the exact college-wise seat distribution directly in the official notification available on ipu.admissions.nic.in.
