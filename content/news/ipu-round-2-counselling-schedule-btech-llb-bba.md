## Round 2 Counselling Activities Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities following the declaration of the Round 2 counselling results. This notification applies to students seeking admission to the B.Tech, LLB, and BBA programmes for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who participated in the second round of counselling must now complete the subsequent admission steps as outlined in the university's official schedule.

## Next Steps for Allotted Candidates

Following the Round 2 seat allotment, candidates are required to perform several key activities within the specified timeline. These typically include:

*   **Checking Allotment Status:** Log in to the official admission portal to view your seat allotment.
*   **Payment of Seat Acceptance Fee:** Candidates who have been allotted a seat for the first time must pay the required fee to confirm their allotment.
*   **Willingness Submission:** Choose whether to 'Freeze' or 'Float' the allotted seat to participate in subsequent rounds.
*   **Document Verification:** Ensure all required documents are verified as per the university guidelines.

For comprehensive guidance on the overall admission process, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). If you are specifically tracking engineering admissions, check our updates on [GGSIPU Counselling for B.Tech Admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) and general [IPU B.Tech Admission](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php) details.

## Important Note on Deadlines

Because post-allotment schedules have strict and limited windows, candidates are strongly advised to download the official PDF notification dated 17.07.2026 directly from the official GGSIPU admissions website (ipu.admissions.nic.in) to verify the exact closing dates and times for fee payment and document submission.
