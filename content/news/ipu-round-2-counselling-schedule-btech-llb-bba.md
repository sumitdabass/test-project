## Round 2 Counselling Activities Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities following the declaration of Round 02 seat allotment results. This schedule applies to candidates seeking admission to the B.Tech, LLB, and BBA programmes for the academic session 2026-27.

Students who participated in the second round of counselling must now complete the subsequent admission formalities within the specified timelines.

## Key Activities for Candidates

Following the declaration of the Round 2 results, candidates are required to perform several critical steps online. To understand the broader process, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### 1. Seat Allotment and Fee Payment
Candidates who have been allotted a seat for the first time in Round 2, or those whose allotment has been upgraded, must pay the part academic fee to confirm their seat. Failure to pay the fee within the stipulated time will result in the cancellation of the allotted seat.

### 2. Willingness Submission (Freeze/Float)
After active seat allotment, candidates must submit their willingness option:
*   **Freeze:** If you are satisfied with the allotted seat and do not wish to participate in further rounds.
*   **Float:** If you want to accept the current seat but wish to be considered for an upgrade in subsequent rounds.

For engineering aspirants, keeping track of these choices is essential for securing a seat via [GGSIPU counselling for B-Tech admission](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php).

### 3. Withdrawal of Admissions
Candidates who wish to withdraw from the counselling process and seek a refund of the part academic fee must do so online within the designated window specified in the official schedule.

## Important Instructions

Since specific deadlines for fee payment, willingness submission, and document verification vary by programme, candidates are strongly advised to download the official schedule PDF dated 17.07.2026 from the official IPU admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in).

Ensure all actions are completed before the respective closing dates to avoid losing your allotted seat. For general information on engineering admissions, you can also check our guide on [IPU B-Tech admission 2025](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php) (updated for the current cycle).
