## Overview of Sports Quota Verification and Trials

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the official timetable for document verification and sports trials for students applying under the sports quota for the 2026-27 academic year.

This verification and trial process applies to all candidates seeking admission through the reserved sports quota seats in the University Schools of Studies (USS) as well as the university's designated Centres of Excellence. Candidates are advised to review the official notification for complete details on schedule dates, reporting times, and venue guidelines, or consult the general [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for additional procedure details.

## Important Instructions for Candidates

- **Verification Requirements:** Applicants must produce all original supporting documents and certificates proving their sports achievements as specified in the university notification.
- **Mandatory Attendance:** Candidates applying under this quota must participate in both the document screening and the physical sports trials according to their designated sport's schedule.
- **Official Notification:** For specific timing and venue breakdowns, candidates should refer directly to the detailed PDF notification published on the official admission portal.
