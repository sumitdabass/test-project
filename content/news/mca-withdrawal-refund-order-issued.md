## MCA Admission Withdrawal Refund Notice

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released a formal refund order for candidates who opted to withdraw their admissions from the Master of Computer Applications (MCA, Code 105) programme for the 2026–27 academic session.

The document has been published under Refund Order No. 3928, Diary No. 3927, dated 10.09.2026.

## Verification Details

Students who applied for withdrawal during the [MCA admission](/mca-admission-ipu.php) process should verify their application records against the published list.

For broader institutional policies regarding admission cancellations and seat processing, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or check the official document on ipu.admissions.nic.in.
