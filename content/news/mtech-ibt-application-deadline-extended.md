## Application Deadline Extended for M.Tech (IBT)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notice extending the final date for submitting online application forms for M.Tech (IBT) (Code-151) for the academic session 2026-27.

Students seeking admission to this program get additional time to complete their registration and submit required details on the admission portal. For step-by-step guidance on university procedures, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Details

- **Course:** M.Tech (IBT)
- **Program Code:** 151
- **Academic Session:** 2026-27
- **Notification Date:** 22 July 2026

Candidates are advised to complete their applications as early as possible. For exact timeline specifics and official notices, please refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
