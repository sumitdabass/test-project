## Spot Round 3 Offline Counselling Schedule Withdrawn

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notice stating that the schedule for Spot Round 3 (Offline) counselling has been withdrawn.

### Official Notification Details

As per the official communication dated August 22, 2026, the schedule previously released vide Notification No. 205/2026 (F.No. IPU-7DI(Academic)/Offline Counselling/2026/2794 dated August 21, 2026) stands withdrawn with immediate effect.

### Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates who were preparing to attend the offline spot round should take note of the cancellation. Prospective students are advised to refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and monitor the official portals (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in) for further instructions or revised schedules regarding the counselling process.
