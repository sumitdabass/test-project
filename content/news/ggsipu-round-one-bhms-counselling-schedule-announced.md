## Round 1 BHMS Counselling Schedule Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the schedule of activities following the declaration of results for Round 01 of the Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS, Code 154) admissions for the academic session 2026–27.

Admissions to this program are being conducted on the basis of NEET UG 2026 scores.

## Next Steps for Allotted Candidates

Following the announcement of Round 01 seat allotment results, candidates participating in the counselling process must complete designated post-result activities according to the university's timeline. These typically include options such as seat acceptance, payment of provisional fees, document verification, and willingness submission.

For general advice on navigating university admission procedures, consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Check Official Notification

Since deadlines for post-allotment procedures are time-sensitive, applicants are urged not to wait. For the comprehensive day-by-day timetable and required steps, refer to the official notification available on the university admissions website at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
