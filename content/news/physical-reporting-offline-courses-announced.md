## Physical Reporting for Offline Courses (2026-27)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification regarding the physical reporting process for candidates seeking admission to various offline courses for the academic session 2026-27. The notification was formally published on July 3, 2026.

### Reporting Requirements

Candidates who are participating in the admission process for offline courses must complete their physical reporting according to the university's designated schedule. 

Because specific reporting dates, timings, and venue details vary depending on the course, candidates are advised to refer directly to the official notification PDF on the GGSIPU admissions portal to avoid missing their slot.

### General Guidelines for Candidates

* Ensure all required original documents and self-attested photocopies are ready before reporting.
* Carry proof of fee payment and the seat allotment letter.
* For a detailed overview of the university's admission procedures, you can check our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
