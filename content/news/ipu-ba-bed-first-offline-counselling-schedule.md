## Overview of BA B.Ed. Offline Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the BA B.Ed. program (CET Code 161) for the 2026-27 academic session. Candidates who have qualified for this program must prepare to attend the counselling process in person.

For a broader understanding of the university's admission procedures, requirements, and general guidelines, you can read our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Key Details and Requirements

Since this is an offline counselling process, candidates must adhere strictly to the schedule based on their ranks. 

### Important Instructions for Candidates

* **Physical Attendance:** Candidates must be physically present at the designated venue to secure their seat allocation.
* **Document Verification:** You must carry all original academic documents, category certificates (if applicable), and the required admission fees for on-the-spot verification.
* **Official Schedule:** For the precise rank-wise reporting times, date slots, and venue details, candidates must refer directly to the official notification PDF published on the GGSIPU admissions portal.
