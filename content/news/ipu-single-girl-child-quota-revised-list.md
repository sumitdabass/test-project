## Revised Candidate List Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published a revised list of applicants who registered under the Single Girl Child Quota for the academic session 2026-27. This list comprises candidates who completed their applications by the stipulated deadline of June 25, 2026.

The official notification was issued on July 8, 2026. Applicants under this category are advised to check the list to verify their details before the next stages of the admission process.

## Key Details of the Announcement

* **Quota Category:** Single Girl Child Quota
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Application Deadline:** June 25, 2026
* **Notification Date:** July 8, 2026

### How to Verify Your Details

Candidates can access the revised list directly on the official GGSIPU admissions portal. It is highly recommended to cross-check all registered details to ensure accuracy for the upcoming counselling rounds. For general information on navigating the university's enrollment process, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

If you find any discrepancies or require further clarification, please refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in or contact the university administration.
