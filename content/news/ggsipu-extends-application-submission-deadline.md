## Application Deadline Extended

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an important notification extending the last date for the submission of online application forms. This extension provides prospective students who missed the initial registration window with another opportunity to apply for their desired programs.

## How to Verify Dates and Apply

Because the exact extended dates depend on the specific course of study, candidates are advised to refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in to confirm the revised schedule for their respective programs.

To assist with your application planning, you can explore our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). If you are seeking entry into specific streams, you may also review the details for [IPU B.Tech admission](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php) or check the requirements for [MBA admission at IP University](/mba-admission-ip-university.php).

## Next Steps for Applicants

* Visit the official GGSIPU admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
* Read the official notification PDF dated June 11, 2026, to verify the exact deadline for your course.
* Complete your online registration, upload the required documents, and pay the application fee before the revised closing date.
