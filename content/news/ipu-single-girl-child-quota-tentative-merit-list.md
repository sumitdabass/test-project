## Single Girl Child Quota Tentative Merit List Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published the tentative merit list for candidates applying under the "Single Girl Child" quota for the current academic session. This quota applies to various undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

### Programs Covered in the Merit List

The tentative merit list has been released for the following courses:
* LLM
* Integrated BA LLB / BBA LLB
* B.Design
* B.Tech (Biotechnology) — For more details on engineering admissions, see the [IPU B.Tech Admission Guide](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php).
* B.Com (Honours)
* BBA
* LLB (3-year)

### Counselling Venue Details for B.Arch, MBA, and M.Arch

For certain programs, the merit list under this quota will not be declared in advance. Instead, the merit lists for the following courses will be prepared directly at **Counselling Venue A**:
* B.Arch
* MBA — For general MBA admission details, refer to the [IPU MBA Admission Guide](/mba-admission-ip-university.php).
* M.Arch

Candidates are advised to check the official GGSIPU admissions portal to verify their details in the tentative list. For any discrepancies or further steps, refer to the official notification. For general admission procedures, you can consult the [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
