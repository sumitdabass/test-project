## BBA CUET Merit List Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the rank and merit list for candidates seeking admission to the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA, Code 125) programme based on their Common University Entrance Test (CUET) scores.

Applicants can consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) for an overview of how merit lists factor into seat allotment.

## Next Steps for Applicants

Students who registered for BBA admissions via CUET scores should check the document available on the admissions portal to verify their rank details. For updates on seat allotment schedules and further participation instructions, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
