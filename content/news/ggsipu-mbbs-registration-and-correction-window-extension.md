## Extension for MBBS (Code 103) Admissions

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has announced an extension of the closing date for fresh registrations and the opening of the edit/correction window for its MBBS programme (Code 103).

Candidates participating in the medical admission process can utilize this extended window to submit new applications or update existing submission details.

## Mandatory Document Uploads

Under this notification, applicants are required to complete the mandatory uploading of all essential credentials, which include:

- Educational certificates and testimonials
- Valid reserved category certificates (where applicable)
- NEET examination scorecards and related documentation

## Actions for Applicants

Eligible medical aspirants who have not yet submitted their forms or need to amend details in their submitted profiles should complete the formalities via the official portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in before the revised deadline. For an overview of university admission procedures, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
