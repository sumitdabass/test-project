## MPO (CET Code 109) First Round Offline Counselling

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the Master of Prosthetics and Orthotics (MPO) program, designated under CET Code 109, for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who have qualified for the respective Common Entrance Test (CET) must participate in this offline process to secure their admission.

## Key Details of the Counselling Process

As this is an offline counselling process, candidates are required to report physically to the designated venue as specified by the university. 

### What to Prepare
*   **Verification of Documents:** Candidates must carry all original documents along with self-attested photocopies for verification.
*   **Fees:** Be prepared with the requisite fees as detailed in the official notification.
*   **Admissions Guide:** For general steps on how IPU conducts its seat allocation, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Instructions for Candidates

Since the schedule has been officially published, candidates are advised to verify their merit rank and report exactly at the designated date and time slot. Failure to appear in person during your allocated slot may result in the forfeiture of your claim to a seat.

For the complete, detailed schedule including reporting times, rank-wise slots, and the counselling venue, please refer directly to the official notification PDF published on the GGSIPU admissions portal.
