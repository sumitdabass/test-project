## First Offline Counselling for MPO (CET Code 109)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the schedule for the first round of offline counselling for the Master of Prosthetics and Orthotics (MPO) program, CET Code 109, for the academic session 2026-27. The official notification was published on June 25, 2026.

Candidates who have qualified in the respective Common Entrance Test (CET) must participate in this physical counselling round to secure their seat allocation.

## Important Counselling Details

Because this is an offline counselling process, candidates are required to report in person. 

*   **Course Name:** Master of Prosthetics and Orthotics (MPO)
*   **CET Code:** 109
*   **Academic Session:** 2026-27
*   **Notification Date:** June 25, 2026

To ensure you do not miss your specific rank-wise slot, please refer to the official PDF notification on the university's admission portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in) for the exact reporting dates, times, venue, and fee payment details. For a broader understanding of the university's entry procedures, you can read our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
