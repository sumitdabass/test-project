## Physical Reporting for Offline Courses

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding the physical reporting process for offline courses for the academic session 2026-27. The notification, dated July 3, 2026, outlines the necessary steps for candidates seeking admission to these programs.

### Key Details and Instructions

Candidates who have been allotted seats or are participating in the offline admission process must note the following:

* **Official Schedule:** The exact dates, timings, and venues for physical reporting vary by course. Candidates must consult the official PDF document published on the university's admission portal.
* **Required Documents:** Ensure you carry all original documents along with self-attested photocopies and the prescribed fees as specified in the official notification.
* **Compliance:** Failure to report physically within the designated timeline may result in the cancellation of the allotted seat.

For a comprehensive overview of the university's admission process, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). Candidates are strongly advised to verify all individual course requirements directly from the official notification at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
