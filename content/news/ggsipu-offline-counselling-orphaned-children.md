## IPU Offline Counselling for Orphaned Children

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the offline counselling schedule for admissions under the supernumerary seats for orphaned children for the academic session 2026-27. 

Candidates eligible under this category must participate in the physical counselling process as per the designated schedule to secure their admission. For a broader understanding of the university's entry procedures, candidates can consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Key Details of the Announcement

* **Category:** Orphaned Children Quota
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Counselling Mode:** Offline
* **Official Notification Date:** July 6, 2026

### Important Instructions for Candidates

Eligible applicants must review the official notification to confirm their specific reporting dates, times, and venue. It is mandatory to bring all required original documents, proof of eligibility under the orphaned category, academic certificates, and the prescribed fee to the counselling venue. 

To stay updated on other admission pathways and past trends, you can review the [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) details.
