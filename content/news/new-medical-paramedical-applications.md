## New Medical and Paramedical Programs Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the introduction of new medical and paramedical programs starting from the 2026-27 academic year. The university has now opened the portal for the submission of online application forms for these specific courses.

## Application Process

Interested candidates are required to register and fill out the application forms via the official GGSIPU admission website. It is important to ensure that all personal and academic details are entered correctly as per the official documents. For a comprehensive look at how to navigate the university's entry process, you can refer to our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Eligibility and Selection

Applicants should carefully review the eligibility criteria for each newly introduced program before applying. Selection for these courses will be based on the university's established norms, which may include entrance tests or merit-based criteria. To understand the competitive landscape for similar healthcare programs, students may find it helpful to review the [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) from previous years.

For the most accurate and detailed information regarding specific course names and seat matrices, students are advised to refer to the official notification available on the university's admission portal.
