## Extended Offline Counselling for B.Pharm (Code 133)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notification regarding the extended open house offline counselling and admission process for the Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) programme, under Programme Code 133, for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates looking for procedural details regarding university admissions can also refer to the general [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Admission Priority and Eligibility Basis

According to the official circular, seat allotment during this extended open house offline round will follow a defined order of merit:

- Seats will first be allotted to candidates qualified through the IPU Common Entrance Test (CET).
- Remaining vacant seats will subsequently be filled based on the merit of the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) only.

### Official Schedule and Venue

Candidates participating in the offline open house must report in person according to the schedule specified by the university. For detailed reporting times, venue locations, and required documentation, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in or ipu.admissions.nic.in.
