## Extended Offline Counselling for B.Pharm (Code 133)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has announced an extended open house offline counselling session for admission to the Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm, Programme Code 133) for the 2026–27 academic session.

## Eligibility and Merit Order

Admissions during this offline open house round will be conducted strictly according to university merit guidelines:

- Primary allotment will be conducted for candidates qualifying via the university's Common Entrance Test (CET).
- Remaining vacant seats, if any, will subsequently be filled based on CUET merit.

Students seeking details on standard counselling norms may review the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and examine historical qualifying thresholds via the [IPU CET cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) page.

## Action Required for Applicants

- This round requires in-person physical attendance at the designated university counselling venue.
- Candidates should ensure they carry their original documents, identification proofs, scorecards, and prescribed fees.
- For exact session timings, reporting schedules, and seat vacancy status, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
