## Official Advisory on Fair Admissions

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification warning prospective students and parents to beware of touts and unauthorized agents promising direct or guaranteed seats for the 2026-27 academic session. 

The university emphasizes that all admissions are conducted strictly through merit-based pathways, including national-level entrance tests, the IPU Common Entrance Test (CET), and the centralized online counselling process. For more details on navigating the admission process safely, you can refer to our comprehensive [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Key Highlights of the Advisory

* **No Third-Party Agents:** GGSIPU has not appointed or authorized any external agencies, consultants, or individuals to facilitate admissions.
* **Merit-Based Selection:** Seats are allocated purely on merit through the designated [GGSIPU Counselling Process](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php).
* **Official Portals Only:** All registration, fee payments, and seat allotments are managed exclusively through the official university portals (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in).

## What Should Students and Parents Do?

To protect yourself from fraudulent activities, always verify any admission-related information directly from the official university website. Do not share personal details, academic certificates, or make financial transactions with individuals claiming to have "management quota" influence or direct access to university officials. If you encounter any suspicious activity, report it to the university authorities immediately.
