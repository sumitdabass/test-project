## Vacant Seats for LLB (Code 121)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification detailing the vacant seat matrix for LLB (Code 121) admissions for the 2026-27 academic session.

Candidates participating in ongoing allotment rounds can review these vacancy numbers to understand remaining seat availability across participating institutes. For general guidance on university procedures and counselling stages, visit the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Checking the Vacancy Details

Applicants should verify institute-wise and category-wise seat vacancies directly through the official notification hosted on ipu.admissions.nic.in. Refer to the university portal for any subsequent counselling schedule instructions.
