## 1st Round Counselling for MPT Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official notification detailing the schedule for the first round of counselling for the Master of Physiotherapy (MPT) programme for the 2026-27 academic session.

The counselling schedule applies to candidates who appeared for CET Code 107. Candidates interested in postgraduate physiotherapy admissions should consult the university portal to review the exact dates, reporting sessions, and documentation requirements.

## Key Notification Details

* **Programme:** Master of Physiotherapy (MPT)
* **CET Code:** 107
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Notification Date:** September 3, 2026
* **Round:** 1st Counselling

### Next Steps for Candidates

Applicants eligible for MPT counselling must review the detailed PDF issued on the portal to confirm their specific reporting slots, category-wise schedules, and mandatory verification procedures. For general information on university admission protocols and procedures, candidates may refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

For official time slots, eligibility criteria, and required paperwork, please refer directly to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in.
