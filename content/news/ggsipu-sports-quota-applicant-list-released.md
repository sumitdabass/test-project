## Sports Quota Applicant List Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the list of candidates who applied for sports quota seats for the 2026-27 academic session. The list encompasses applicants seeking entry into various undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) courses.

## Coverage and Departments

The released list covers applicants targeting programs across:
- University Schools of Studies (USS)
- Centres of Excellence at GGSIPU

Students seeking updates on general entry procedures can review the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Candidate Verification

Applicants should carefully check their names and application details in the document. For further announcements regarding verification schedules or trial dates, candidates must refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
