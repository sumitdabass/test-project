## Post-Round 1 Counselling Schedule Released

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities to be followed after the declaration of Round 1 counselling results. This schedule applies to the academic session 2026-27 and was published on July 2, 2026.

Candidates who participated in the first round of counselling for the specified undergraduate and postgraduate courses must now follow the subsequent procedures, such as fee payment, seat freezing or floating, and document verification.

## Programs Covered Under This Schedule

The newly released schedule applies to the following courses:
* **B.Tech** (For detailed guidance, check the [GGSIPU B.Tech Counselling Guide](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) and the [IPU B.Tech Admission Portal](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php))
* **BBA** & **5-Year BBA-MBA (Integrated)**
* **BCA**
* **LLM**

## Key Actions for Candidates

Since the university has not detailed all individual program deadlines in the main headline, candidates are strongly advised to download the official PDF from the IPU admissions website to avoid missing critical deadlines. 

Typical post-allotment steps include:
1. **Checking Allotment Status:** Log in to the GGSIPU admission portal to view your seat allotment.
2. **Payment of Part Academic Fee:** Pay the required fee to confirm your allotted seat within the stipulated timeframe.
3. **Willingness Submission:** Choose to "Freeze" your seat if satisfied, or "Float" if you wish to participate in subsequent rounds.

For a comprehensive overview of the university's admission process, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). Always verify the exact dates and instructions on the official portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
