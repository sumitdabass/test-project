## BA (English) Admission Withdrawal Refund Notice

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official refund order for applicants who withdrew their admission from the BA (English) programme (CET Code 184) for the academic session 2026-27.

### Key Details of the Order

* **Programme Name:** BA (English)
* **CET Code:** 184
* **Academic Session:** 2026-27
* **Order Reference:** Refund Order No. 3929 (Diary No. 3928)
* **Order Date:** September 10, 2026

Candidates who completed the withdrawal process for this course can review the official circular on the university admission portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in) to verify their details and track the refund status.
