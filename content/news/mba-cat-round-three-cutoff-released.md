## Round 3 MBA Cutoffs Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official Round 03 cutoff data for candidates seeking admission to its Master of Business Administration (MBA) programme through CAT scores (Code 101).

The notification outlines the opening and closing ranks across participating institutes and categories following the conclusion of the third allotment round. Prospective and participating students can review these ranks to gauge institutional preferences and allocation trends.

## Checking MBA Cutoffs and Next Steps

Candidates participating in the seat allocation process can check the college-wise minimum and maximum ranks on the university admissions portal (ipu.admissions.nic.in). 

For broader context on seat allotment, eligibility, and subsequent stages, review our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and explore detailed steps in the [MBA admission process at IP University](/mba-admission-ip-university.php). Students comparing past trends across programmes can also inspect previous standards via the [IPU cutoff reference page](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php).

For official document verification and fee payment instructions following Round 3 allotments, candidates must refer directly to the notifications published on the university website.
