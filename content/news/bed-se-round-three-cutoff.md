## B.Ed (Special Education) Round 3 Cutoff Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the Round 3 cutoff ranks for admission to the B.Ed Special Education programme (CET Code 159).

Candidates who participated in the third round of counselling can now review the minimum and maximum closing ranks across participating institutes.

## Accessing the Cutoff Details

The cutoff notification provides institute-wise and category-wise closing ranks for Round 3 allotment. Prospective students can access the official PDF document directly through the admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in.

For more information on general university counselling steps, check the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Allotted Candidates

Candidates participating in the seat allotment rounds should:

* Check the official portal to verify seat allotment results against the published closing ranks.
* Refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in for complete details on subsequent counselling instructions and verification schedules.
