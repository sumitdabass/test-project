## Round 3 Paramedical Cutoff Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has officially issued the Round 3 cutoff data for admissions into its allied health and paramedical programs under CET Code 124. Candidates who took part in this counselling stage can now review the closing ranks across participating institutes.

For broader context on seat allotment procedures, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and explore previous rank benchmarks through the [IPU cutoff reference page](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php).

### Included Courses

The Round 3 cutoff list applies to the following Paramedical degree programs:

- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT)
- Bachelor of Prosthetics and Orthotics (BPO)
- Bachelor in Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)
- Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Technology (BSc MLT)
- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (BOT)

### Verifying Cutoff Details

All category-wise and institute-wise opening and closing ranks are detailed in the official PDF uploaded to ipu.admissions.nic.in. Candidates should check the official document to verify their eligibility status for subsequent counselling phases.
