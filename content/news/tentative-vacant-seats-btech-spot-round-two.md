## B.Tech Spot Round 2 Vacant Seats Published

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the tentative vacancy status for Spot Round 02 of [B.Tech admissions](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php) (Programme Code 131). The list outlines the availability of seats across affiliated institutes as on August 14, 2026.

## Key Details for Applicants

Students participating in [GGSIPU counselling for B.Tech](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) should check the seat matrix to assess available options across institutions and branches for this round.

### Important Considerations

* **Tentative Status:** The seat numbers are tentative and subject to changes as per counselling procedures.
* **Programme Specifics:** The notification applies strictly to B.Tech Programme Code 131.
* **Official Verification:** For institute-wise and branch-wise seat distributions, refer to the official document on ipu.admissions.nic.in or review general procedures via the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
