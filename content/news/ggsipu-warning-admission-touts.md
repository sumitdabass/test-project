## GGSIPU Issues Warning Against Fake Admission Promises

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official advisory warning students and parents to remain vigilant against unauthorized individuals or touts promising guaranteed admissions for the academic session 2026-27. 

The university emphasizes that all admissions are conducted strictly through the official channels and established merit-based procedures.

## Key Highlights of the Advisory

* **No Third-Party Agents:** GGSIPU has not authorized any external agencies, consultants, or individuals to facilitate admissions or guarantee seats in any program.
* **Official Channels Only:** Candidates must refer only to the official university websites (ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in) for all admission-related processes, registration, and counselling schedules.
* **Merit-Based Process:** Admissions to undergraduate and postgraduate programs are based on merit, determined by national-level tests, the university's Common Entrance Test (CET), or qualifying exams.

## How to Protect Yourself

To ensure a safe and legitimate application process, prospective students should follow the official [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). 

* Do not share personal details, login credentials, or original documents with unverified individuals.
* Avoid making financial transactions to personal bank accounts or cash payments to individuals claiming to represent the university.
* Always verify admission notices directly on the official university portal.
