## LLM Round 1 Counselling Update

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notification regarding the 1-year Master of Law (LL.M.) program, designated under Program Code 112. Certain candidates have been debarred from participating in the Round 1 counselling and seat allotment process.

## Reason for Debarment

According to the university's announcement, the debarment is a result of the non-validation of the candidates' CLAT PG 2026 ranks. Successful verification of national-level exam ranks is a mandatory prerequisite for seat allotment. Those whose ranks could not be authenticated during the verification stage are excluded from this round.

### What Affected Candidates Should Do

If you have applied for the LLM program at GGSIPU for the 2026 session, you must immediately check the official PDF released on the university's admission portal.

* **Verify Status:** Check the official list of debarred candidates to confirm your status.
* **Official Portal:** Visit ipu.admissions.nic.in to access the complete list and read the detailed instructions.
* **Further Guidance:** For general information on university admission procedures, you can consult our [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
