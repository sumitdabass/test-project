## Spot Round 2 Counselling Announced for Law Programmes

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the official notification regarding the schedule for Spot Round 02 counselling for admissions into integrated law programmes, including BA LLB and BBA LLB, for the 2026–27 academic session.

### Mode of Counselling

According to the official release, the counselling process will proceed in two stages:
- **Online Spot Round 02**: An initial online round for eligible candidates.
- **Offline Round**: A subsequent in-person round following the conclusion of the online process.

### Important Considerations for Applicants

Prospective law students looking for general admission updates can refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). Candidates seeking admission should review the official document available on the university admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in for precise registration deadlines, seat conversion rules, and reporting details.
