## Extension of Application Deadline for New Medical & Paramedical Programmes

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released an official notification regarding the extension of the last date to submit online application forms for its newly introduced medical and paramedical programmes. This extension applies to the academic session 2026-27.

### Affected Programmes

The extension is specifically applicable to:
* Newly introduced Paramedical programmes
* Newly introduced Medical programmes

### What Candidates Should Do

Prospective students who wish to apply for these newly launched courses now have additional time to complete and submit their online application forms. 

Because the exact revised deadline date is subject to the specific details in the official university release, candidates are strongly advised to refer to the official notification dated June 1, 2026, available on the official IPU admission portal to confirm the final submission date.

For a comprehensive overview of the university's general admission process, requirements, and other course details, you can consult our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
