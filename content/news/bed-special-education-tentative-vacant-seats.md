## Vacant Seat Details for B.Ed Special Education

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notification detailing the tentative vacant seats for admission into the B.Ed (Special Education) programme, CET Code 159, for the 2026-27 academic session.

The seat status gives participating candidates an update on the remaining availability across affiliated institutes before subsequent counselling rounds proceed. Candidates planning their choices can review general eligibility and counselling protocols through the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Next Steps for Candidates

Applicants seeking a seat in B.Ed (Special Education) should:

- Visit the official admissions portal at `ipu.admissions.nic.in` to access the complete PDF document.
- Review the institute-wise and category-wise distribution of remaining seats.
- Keep track of upcoming counselling activity schedules and seat allotment instructions published by the university.
