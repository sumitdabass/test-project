## Application Process Withdrawn for M.Sc. SLP

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the withdrawal of online application form submissions for the **M.Sc. Speech Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP)** program, which is registered under CET Code 168. 

The official notification, dated May 19, 2026, confirms that the online application process for this specific course has been suspended. 

## What Should Candidates Do?

Prospective students who intended to apply for the M.Sc. SLP program must take note of this update. No further submissions are being accepted for Code 168 at this time. 

For students looking to apply to other postgraduate or undergraduate courses at the university, please consult our detailed [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) to understand the active registration steps and requirements. For specific queries regarding the withdrawal of the M.Sc. SLP application, candidates should refer to the official notifications published on the GGSIPU website at ipu.ac.in.
