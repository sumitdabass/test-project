## Revised Seat Matrix for B.Ed. (Special Education)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued an official notice detailing the revised sanctioned intake for its B.Ed. (Special Education) programme for the academic session 2026-27.

The programme, designated under Programme Code 159, has had its seat distribution updated across participating institutes. For general information on university admission rules and seat allocation processes, consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Inclusion of Management Quota Seats

The revised notice specifies the sanctioned intake counts across categories, including the allocated Management Quota seats for eligible colleges.

### Accessing the Official Breakdown

Applicants can review the official institution-wise seat numbers and reservation details directly on the university admissions portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in. Candidates are advised to review the official notification dated October 8, 2026, for precise college-by-college breakdowns.
