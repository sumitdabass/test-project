## Vacant Seat List for BA Economics (Code 197)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has published the official list of vacant seats for admission to the Bachelor of Arts in Economics program, designated under course code 197, for the 2026–27 academic session.

The document details remaining vacancies across participating colleges following earlier admission rounds. Candidates tracking further rounds or spot counselling should consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) to understand how vacant seats are typically allocated.

## Next Steps for Applicants

Students seeking admission to BA (Economics) should verify category-wise and institute-wise seat availability through the published list. For the complete PDF document and further schedule updates, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
