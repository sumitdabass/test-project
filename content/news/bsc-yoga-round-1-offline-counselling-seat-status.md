## GGSIPU B.Sc Yoga (Code 187) Seat Status Update

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially published the seat status for the B.Sc (Yoga) program, CET Code 187, following the completion of the first round of offline counselling for the 2026-2027 academic session. 

Candidates who participated in the initial round or are planning to attend subsequent offline admission rounds can now verify the remaining vacant seats across participating institutes.

## How to Check the Vacant Seats

The university has uploaded the detailed seat matrix as a PDF document on the official admissions portal. Since seat availability changes dynamically during offline counselling, candidates are advised to review the official document before preparing for the next steps.

To view the official seat status:
1. Visit the official IPU admissions website at ipu.admissions.nic.in.
2. Navigate to the "Current Events" section.
3. Click on the link for "B.Sc(YOGA)_187 Seat Status after the end of 1st Round of offline Counselling".

For comprehensive details on navigating the university's admission process, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php). If you are looking for previous opening and closing ranks to estimate your chances, check out the [IPU CET Cutoff](/ipu-cet-cutoff-2025.php) details.

## Next Steps for Candidates

Candidates waiting for subsequent rounds of offline counselling should keep a close watch on the official university website (ipu.ac.in) for schedule announcements regarding Round 2 or spot counselling. Ensure you have all necessary documents, including your CET admit card, rank card, and academic certificates, ready for verification.
