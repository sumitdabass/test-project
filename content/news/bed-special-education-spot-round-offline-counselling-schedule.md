## Spot Round Offline Counselling Announced

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notification detailing the offline spot round counselling schedule for admissions to the B.Ed (Special Education) programme (CET Code 159) for the academic session 2026-27.

Prospective candidates participating in this round should review the official timeline and instructions carefully before attending the in-person counselling sessions. For general procedures regarding university admissions, see the [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Important Information for Candidates

- **Programme:** Bachelor of Education (Special Education) [B.Ed (SE)]
- **CET Code:** 159
- **Mode of Counselling:** Offline
- **Academic Session:** 2026–27

Candidates must carry all prescribed documents, certificates, and requisite fees according to the official guidelines released by the university. For exact reporting dates, reporting venues, and round-wise eligibility criteria, refer to the official notification at ipu.ac.in.
