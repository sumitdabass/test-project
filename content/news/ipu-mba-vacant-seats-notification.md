## Vacant Seats Published for MBA (CET Code 101)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued a notification detailing the available vacant seats for its Master of Business Administration (MBA) programme under CET Code 101.

Prospective management students participating in the ongoing counselling process can refer to this list to assess seat availability across participating institutes and university schools. To learn more about course eligibility and standard procedures, check our [MBA admission guide](/mba-admission-ip-university.php).

## Checking Seat Availability

The university provides institute-wise and category-wise seat vacancy metrics to help applicants make informed choices during seat allocation rounds. Candidates are advised to review the official PDF released on the admissions portal before locking their preferences.

For a complete overview of the overall admission process, refer to the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) or verify updates directly at ipu.ac.in.
