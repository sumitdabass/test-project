## Round 1 Activities Announced for LE B.Tech (Diploma)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has officially released the schedule of activities following the declaration of the Round 1 seat allotment results for the Lateral Entry to B.Tech (Diploma) program, CET Code 128, for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who participated in this round of counselling must now proceed with the post-allotment steps according to the university's specified timeline.

## Key Post-Allotment Steps

Following the seat allotment result, candidates are generally required to complete the following steps within the designated window:

*   **Payment of Seat Acceptance Fee:** Candidates who have been allotted a seat must pay the required fee online to secure their allotment.
*   **Submission of Willingness:** Candidates must choose to either 'Freeze' their allotted seat if they are satisfied, or 'Float' if they wish to participate in subsequent rounds for a better option.
*   **Withdrawal of Admissions:** Candidates who wish to withdraw from the counselling process must do so within the specified time frame to be eligible for a refund as per university norms.

For general information regarding engineering admissions, you can refer to our guide on [IPU B.Tech Admission](/IPU-B-Tech-admission-2025.php) or read more about the overall [GGSIPU Counselling Process](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php).

## Important Notice for Candidates

Because deadlines for post-allotment activities are strictly enforced, candidates are strongly advised to refer directly to the official notification PDF on the university's admission portal to verify the exact dates and times for fee submission, document verification, and seat freezing. Failure to complete these activities within the stipulated time will result in the cancellation of the allotted seat.
