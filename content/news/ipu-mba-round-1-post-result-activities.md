## Post-Allotment Activities for MBA (Code 101)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has released the schedule of activities to be followed after the declaration of the Round 1 seat allotment results for the MBA (Code 101) program for the academic session 2026-27. 

Candidates who participated in the first round of counselling must complete the subsequent steps within the designated timeline to secure their allotted seats. For a comprehensive overview of the process, you can refer to our [IPU Admission Guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

## Key Steps After Round 1 Allotment

According to the university's standard procedure, candidates must log in to the portal to perform several critical actions once results are live:

*   **Check Allotment Status:** Log in to the GGSIPU admission portal to view your seat allotment result.
*   **Submit Willingness:** Choose whether to 'Freeze' the allotted seat if you are satisfied, or 'Float' to participate in subsequent rounds.
*   **Pay Part Academic Fee:** Pay the required seat acceptance fee online within the specified window to retain the seat.
*   **Print Allotment Letter:** Download and print the provisional allotment letter for verification purposes.

For more details on the MBA admission process and college options, check out our guide on [MBA Admission in IP University](/mba-admission-ip-university.php).

## Important Instructions

Candidates are advised to read the official notification dated 10.07.2026 carefully. Missing any deadline for fee payment or willingness submission will lead to automatic cancellation of the allotted seat, and the candidate will lose the right to participate in subsequent online counselling rounds. Refer to the official portal at ipu.admissions.nic.in to access the complete PDF and schedule.
