## Application Process Suspended for M.Sc. SLP (Code 168)

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has announced the withdrawal of the online application form submission process for the M.Sc. Speech Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP) program, which is registered under CET Code 168.

The official notification regarding this decision was released on May 19, 2026.

### What Applicants Need to Know

Prospective students who intended to apply for the M.Sc. SLP program for the current academic session will no longer be able to submit their applications online. 

Because the official notification does not specify the reason for the withdrawal or outline the next steps for candidates who may have already submitted their details, applicants must monitor the university's official portals for subsequent updates. 

For information regarding other active courses, eligibility criteria, and general registration procedures, please consult our comprehensive [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).
