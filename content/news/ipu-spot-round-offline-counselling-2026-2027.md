## Spot Round Offline Counselling Notice

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has published an official notification dated 07 August 2026 regarding the spot round offline counselling for the academic session 2026–2027.

Candidates participating in the university's admission process can refer to the general [ipu admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php) and check specific guidelines on [GGSIPU counselling](/GGSIPU-counselling-for-B-Tech-admission.php) for further process details.

### Key Details

- **Notification Date:** 07.08.2026
- **Academic Session:** 2026–2027
- **Mode:** Offline Counselling

For exact reporting times, venue details, eligible candidate lists, and required documents, candidates should refer to the official notification available on ipu.admissions.nic.in.
