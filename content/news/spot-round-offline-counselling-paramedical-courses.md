## Spot Round Offline Counselling for Paramedical Programs

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) has issued the schedule for the offline spot round of counselling for various undergraduate paramedical courses for the academic session 2026-27.

Candidates who appeared for CET 2026 or CUET 2026 and wish to secure admission into affiliated paramedical colleges are eligible to participate as per the university guidelines. For broader information regarding entrance and counselling procedures, candidates may consult the [IPU admission guide](/ipu-admission-guide.php).

### Courses Covered

The offline spot counselling encompasses the following paramedical degree programmes:

- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (B.OT)
- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.PT)
- Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)
- Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Sciences (BMLS)
- Bachelor of Prosthetics and Orthotics (BPO)

### Important Instructions

- **Mode of Counselling:** The spot round will be conducted strictly in offline mode.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Seats will be allocated to qualified candidates holding valid scores in CET 2026 or CUET 2026.
- **Detailed Schedule & Venue:** Candidates must consult the official notification issued on October 16, 2026, at ipu.admissions.nic.in or ipu.ac.in for venue details, exact reporting timings, and document requirements.
